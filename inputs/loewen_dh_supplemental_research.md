## Air Infiltration — A3 Rating Explanation
A3 is the HIGHEST (best) air infiltration level under the Canadian supplement to NAFS:
- A1 = 2.78 L/(s·m²) at 75 Pa — worst
- A2 = 1.65 L/(s·m²) at 75 Pa — moderate  
- A3 = 0.55 L/(s·m²) at 75 Pa — best possible

ALL Loewen DH products tested at A3 level. This is equivalent to excellent air infiltration performance.
