# Baseline Research: Noritz (tankless water heating)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT scored — water heater calibration exists in repo but category needs confirmation)

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** Noritz America Corp. Japanese parent; building tankless since 1981.
**Position:** one of the leading tankless brands alongside Rinnai, Takagi, Rheem. Typical unit $1,200-3,200 before installation.

## MARKET PLACEMENT (Residentialist database)
- 3 mentions total. Essentially absent from listing text.
- Interpretation: water heaters are almost never listed. This says nothing about the brand. (Standing ruling: absence from listing text is not a signal.)

## WARRANTY — THE STANDOUT
- 12 years replacement on the heat exchanger in single-family use (from Noritz's own warranty terms)
- Up to 25 years on select models (EZ line) per trade sources — well beyond the roughly 12-year category norm
- Parts typically ~5 years; limited labor at a reasonable rate paid to authorized service representatives
- Warranty takes effect at installation by a Noritz Authorized Installer, or 30 days after purchase, whichever is first
- Competitive frame: Takagi 15-year limited, Rheem 12-year. Noritz's term is the most confident in the category.

## SERVICE REALITY
- Trade/installer sources rate Noritz a preferred brand with a good contractor reputation
- Expected service life 15-20 years with proper install and annual maintenance
- Maintenance dependency is real: annual descaling required, more often in hard water. **Central Texas water is hard** — this is a genuine ownership caveat for this house and a good buyer-education beat.
- Sizing is the other variable: GPM must match peak demand (large homes often need 9-11+ GPM)

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- Warranty term is the strongest in category — a real quality signal, not marketing
- Non-condensing vs condensing model matters for efficiency; unknown here
- Watch for source quality: much of the available "review" content is installer marketing. Weight the manufacturer warranty document and trade consensus over blog roundups.
