# Baseline Research: Carrier heat pump (HVAC)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT scored — HVAC category rubric does not exist yet)

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** Carrier. Three residential tiers: Comfort (entry/mid), Performance (mid), Infinity (premium, Greenspeed variable-speed).
**Market position:** reliable mainstream brand, wide dealer network, good parts availability.

## MARKET PLACEMENT (Residentialist database)
- 94 mentions total, 21 Texas. Curve sits low: peaks in the $1-2M band, only 8 mentions above $3.5M.
- Interpretation caution: HVAC is under-reported in listing text generally (same limitation as envelope), so low mention count is weak evidence about tier. Do NOT read as "cheap brand."

## PERFORMANCE (Infinity Greenspeed, published/AHRI)
- SEER2 20, HSPF2 11.5 versus federal minimums of 14.3 and 7.5
- Variable-speed modulating compressor; operates to -15F, COP 2.1 at 5F (cold-climate spec, largely irrelevant in Austin)
- Outdoor unit ~52 dB
- Full replacement projects run roughly $6,000-25,000 depending on tier, home size, ductwork

## WARRANTY
- Infinity Greenspeed: 10-year compressor, 5-year parts
- Registration required within 90 days of installation to activate full coverage — a real trap; unregistered systems drop to base terms
- Extended coverage available through authorized dealers

## SERVICE REALITY / THE CENTRAL FINDING
Every independent source converges on the same point: with Carrier, installation quality dominates equipment tier. A well-installed midrange unit outperforms a poorly installed premium one. Named determinants: Manual J sizing, refrigerant charge accuracy, commissioning, duct sizing and sealing, insulation. Leaky or undersized ducts negate the efficiency the equipment is rated for.

**Why this matters for this house specifically:** the Tom Miller story is envelope and mechanicals (ZIP, BIBS/foam, sealed vs vented attic, ducts in conditioned space). The literature says the brand on the box is the least interesting fact about the system. The walk questions — where the foam is, whether the ducts are inside the envelope, how it was commissioned — are the actual story. Carrier is a competent mainstream choice; the install is the variable.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- Which Carrier tier is installed matters more than the brand: Comfort vs Performance vs Infinity is a large spread
- 10-year compressor / 5-year parts is standard, not premium, for the category
- Registration-dependent coverage is a consumer trap worth naming in buyer-facing content
