## CRITICAL CONTEXT
Marvin Signature Ultimate is Marvin's TOP-TIER aluminum-clad wood product line. The DH G2 is the current-generation double hung. This is a CLAD product — extruded aluminum exterior over solid wood interior. Bot 1 must correctly classify this as aluminum-clad wood, NOT bare wood.

## Wood Species & Construction
Source: https://www.marvin.com/f/1019562/x/ef4dc460fd/2024-marvin-ultimate_technical_specifications-catalog_19980961.pdf

- Frame width: 4-9/16" (116mm)
- Sash construction: Solid wood core
- Weather strip: Foam-filled bulb on jambs (beige, black, white options)
- Balance: Block & tackle (lighter sashes) or Hybrid spiral (heavier)
- Sash operation: Both sashes tilt in 90° for cleaning
- Auto-lock: Exclusive feature — sash automatically locks when closed
