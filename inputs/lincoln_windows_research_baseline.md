# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT yet scored — feed to scorer in Claude Code)
# Source policy: manufacturer docs + dealer literature + consumer review aggregators. Manufacturer claims labeled as such.

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** Lincoln Wood Products, Inc. (LWP)
**Origin:** Merrill, Wisconsin. Privately owned, in wood windows for over half a century.
**Distribution:** ~400 independent distributors across US/Canada. Not a big-box brand.
**Material class:** Aluminum-clad wood (also primed wood, all wood; 7 interior species)
**History note:** Entered vinyl via Timeline Vinyl Products; that arm closed 2015 after losses. Core wood/clad business continues.

## CONSTRUCTION / PERFORMANCE
- Heavy 0.050 extruded aluminum cladding over solid wood frame and sash
- Mortise-and-tenon joinery, double-strength glass standard (dealer literature)
- AAMA 2605 exterior finish (top-tier fenestration coating standard; many competitors stop at 2604)
- Glass packages: double-pane Low-E, triple-pane, argon fill, tinted/specialty coatings
- Color program: 8 standard, 38 feature, 7 anodized, plus custom lab color match

## WARRANTY (from LWP warranty PDFs)
- General product: 10-year limited from date of manufacture
- Aluminum-clad finish: 20-year limited
- Insulated glass / triple pane: 20-year
- Interior finish: 2-year limited
- Hardware: 10 years against defects; hardware FINISHES not warranted
- **Non-transferable**, proof of purchase required
- Exclusions: clad damage from cleaning/powerwash/brickwash/chemicals/salt/acid rain/airborne pollutants; condensation and resulting water damage; improper or incomplete finishing (all wood surfaces must be painted or stained); products not installed per LWP instructions; post-installation compliance with published air/water/structural/energy ratings; argon fill rates over time; consequential/incidental damages (capped at purchase price)
- Heat-reflective paint required on exterior cPVC components at L-value 55 or darker

## SERVICE REALITY
- Yelp ~2.5 across a small review count (24). Aggregator commentary notes wood-clad brands generally draw poor reviews because failures surface in the second decade, and that clad lines carry shorter warranties than vinyl for that reason.
- Reviewer consensus (replacementwindowsreviews.co, June 2026): good-quality wood and clad product today, solid reputation among dealers and builders, spotty consumer-satisfaction record.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- 10-year general term is SHORT versus the 20-year-glass/lifetime-frame norm at the premium clad tier (Marvin Signature, Andersen E-Series, Loewen all carry 20-year glass)
- AAMA 2605 finish is a genuine premium marker (matches Loewen, Marvin Signature)
- Non-transferability is a real negative in a resale-oriented luxury market
- Small-distributor model: service depends heavily on the local dealer
