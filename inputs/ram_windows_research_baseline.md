# Baseline Research: RAM Windows & Doors (thermally broken aluminum)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT yet scored)

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** RAM Industries, Inc. — Texas. Operating since 1978, 30+ years serving architects, builders, remodelers, developers.
**Structure:** Both manufacturer AND distributor of its own windows and doors (their own stated differentiator)
**Markets:** San Antonio, Austin, Houston, Dallas. Residential work described as custom homes typically $500K and up.
**Material class:** All-aluminum, thermally broken. Core competency stated as thermally broken aluminum systems.
**Products:** Windows, sliding, folding, swing, and pivot doors; large-format multi-panel systems. Offers Cardinal CLiC switchable privacy glass on select windows and hinged doors. Has a program aimed at builders doing 20+ homes a year.

## CONSTRUCTION / PERFORMANCE
- 6063-T5 extruded aluminum, thermally broken
- Finishes: high-performance paint at AAMA 2604 or 2605 grade, or anodized Class I / Class II
- Explicitly engineered for Texas conditions (heat, large glass openings)
- Company price guidance: roughly $300-1,200 per window, $600-1,500 per door

## WARRANTY (company FAQ)
- 10 years from original delivery for single-family residential
- 2 years for commercial / non-single-family
- Claims route through the dealer, architect, or builder who placed the order; direct purchasers deal with RAM
- Modifications that contribute to a defect void coverage for that defect

## SERVICE REALITY
- Local-expert service model is the company's stated differentiator: regional presence for expedited response
- Independent review coverage is thin and largely directory-grade; no substantial BBB complaint pattern surfaced in this pass
- **Evidence gap flag:** insufficient independent service data to score service confidently. Scorer should mark low confidence on serviceability rather than infer from marketing.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- AAMA 2604-or-2605 range means finish quality varies by order; 2604 is a step below the 2605 premium marker
- 10-year term matches Lincoln, below the 20-year glass norm of premium clad-wood
- Regional manufacturer with in-market service is a real structural advantage in Texas, and a real risk outside it
- The stated custom-home floor ($500K and up) spans a wide range, so brand presence alone does not signal luxury tier the way Fleetwood does
