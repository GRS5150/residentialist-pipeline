# Andersen E-Series DH — Supplemental Research: Performance Certifications & NAFS Test Data
# Source: andersenwindows.com, Andersen E-Series Product Guide, NFRC data — verified March 13, 2026

## CRITICAL CONTEXT
Andersen E-Series (formerly Eagle brand, acquired by Andersen) is their PREMIUM custom architectural line. This is a CLAD product — extruded aluminum exterior over solid wood interior. It competes directly with Marvin Signature Ultimate and Loewen. Bot 1 must correctly classify this as aluminum-clad wood, NOT bare wood or vinyl-clad. E-Series is ABOVE the A-Series and 400 Series in Andersen's hierarchy.
