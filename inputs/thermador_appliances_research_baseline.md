# Baseline Research: Thermador (appliances)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT scored — appliances category rubric does not exist yet)

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** Thermador, kitchen appliances since 1916. Owned by BSH (Bosch group) — Bosch/Thermador appear together in service and warranty complaints.
**Products in scope:** built-in ovens, cooktops, refrigerators, dishwashers, downdraft ventilation
**Positioning:** professional-grade features for residential kitchens; suite packages commonly $8K-25K+

## MARKET PLACEMENT (Residentialist database)
- 5,284 mentions after cleanup, curve peaks in the $1-2M band
- Sub-Zero (7,352) and Wolf (7,342) both peak higher. At $2M+ Thermador is the deliberate step down from the Sub-Zero/Wolf default.

## SERVICE REALITY — THE DOMINANT FINDING
Consumer complaint volume is high and the pattern is consistent across ConsumerAffairs, Trustpilot, and PissedConsumer (2025-2026 reviews):
- Parts backorders and multi-month waits; one documented case 3+ months without a working microwave after a 4-year failure
- Repeated component replacement without resolution: a downdraft replaced 4 times under warranty; control/display boards awaiting parts 6+ months; one oven part replaced 3 times, still non-functional
- Warranty administration: original proof of purchase demanded even when the product was registered at install; a Florida AG complaint filed over an unhonored warranty
- Recurring failure modes named by owners: ovens slow to reach or failing to hold temperature, refrigerator ice buildup and airflow loss, ice makers failing shortly after warranty, moisture between oven/microwave door glass that cannot be cleaned
- Warranty term criticized as short relative to unit lifespan, labor not covered
- One owner reported replacing a Thermador refrigerator with a Sub-Zero and rating the Sub-Zero clearly superior

**Counterweight for fairness:** ConsumerAffairs/Trustpilot/PissedConsumer are complaint-selecting venues; satisfied owners rarely post. Volume and consistency are the signal, not any single review. Score service on pattern, not anecdote count.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- Strong design/feature reputation, weak service reputation. Classic split.
- Builder-channel favorite (one review notes the builder's own home was wall-to-wall Thermador) — relevant to how it lands in spec sheets
- The gap between purchase price and post-warranty support is the buyer-relevant finding
