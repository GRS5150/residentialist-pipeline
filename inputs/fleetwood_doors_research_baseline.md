# Baseline Research: Fleetwood Windows & Doors (aluminum, sliding/multi-slide/pivot)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT yet scored)

## PRODUCT OVERVIEW
**Manufacturer:** Fleetwood Aluminum Products, Inc. — Corona, California. Manufacturing modern doors and windows since 1961.
**Material class:** All-aluminum, exclusively. Thermally broken frames on current lines.
**Lines:** 3-Series (elite luxury), Gen4 (evolved from 3-Series, coastal/extreme-weather focus, flow-through thresholds, jumbo glass), EDGE (ultra-slim, largest sliding panels in the industry per dealer literature)
**Positioning:** Market leader in luxury large-format sliding, multi-slide, pocket, and pivot systems. Made to order.

## CONSTRUCTION / PERFORMANCE
- Thermally broken aluminum; high-efficiency glass packages
- Finishes: paint and anodizing, both inert; no refinishing expected
- Gen4 marketed for coastal environments with air, water, and structural performance ratings called out by dealers
- Sustainability: recycled aluminum and glass, 100% aluminum scrap recycled

## WARRANTY (dealer-published; VERIFY against factory doc before printing)
- Fleetwood advertises a transferable lifetime warranty and calls it the industry's only true one (manufacturer claim)
- Dealer-published breakdown: frame finish (exterior and interior) 20 years, glass 10 years, hardware lifetime/replaceable, coastal 20 years
- **Conflict flag:** "transferable lifetime" marketing versus a component schedule with 10-year glass. Scorer should treat the component schedule as governing and the lifetime claim as marketing until the factory document is obtained.
- Dealer warning in circulation: some products carry no factory warranty unless installed by an authorized dealer. Get it in writing.

## SERVICE REALITY
- BBB reviews contain substantive complaints: an inside-pane spontaneous glass failure at 18 months denied as "does not look like spontaneous breakage" with subsequent messages unanswered; a separate multi-year unresolved distributor-caused issue with air leakage.
- Counter-evidence: long-tenure owner reports (15 years on sliders) praising warranty and service, and repair-trade sources rating EDGE best in class against European lookalikes.
- Pattern to score: dealer/distributor-dependent service with a wide spread between excellent and non-responsive, similar in shape to other dealer-network brands already in the table.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- Genuine luxury tier; the database confirms concentration in the top price bands
- Aluminum-only means high strength and slim sightlines, but thermal performance depends entirely on the thermal break and glass package. In Texas heat, that is the question that matters.
- Glass at 10 years is BELOW the 20-year glass norm of the premium clad-wood set
