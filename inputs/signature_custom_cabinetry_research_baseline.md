# Baseline Research: Signature Custom Cabinetry (cabinetry)
# Compiled: 2026-09-04 (chat session; NOT scored — no cabinetry rubric)

## MANUFACTURER
Signature Custom Cabinetry, Inc. — Ephrata, Pennsylvania. Founded 1989 by Kent Martin and his brothers; Martin came out of Conestoga Wood Specialties. ~43 employees, ~$21.9M revenue (2026). KCMA member.
**Channel:** sold only through a nationwide network of independent authorized dealers and certified kitchen designers. Not available direct.
**Self-positioning (CEO, on record):** aiming to be the best value in the high-end custom segment — explicitly not the ultra-luxury one-of-a-kind veneer tier.

## CONSTRUCTION
- Interlocking French dovetail construction
- Hand-wiped stain
- Regional context: Pennsylvania Amish/Mennonite fine-woodworking tradition; some employees come from it
- Documented standard work per process for quality control, with associates trained against the written standard rather than personal method (CEO on record)

## WARRANTY
- Lifetime Limited Warranty; details only through the dealer/designer. **Terms not published publicly — obtain the document before printing anything about coverage.**

## SERVICE REALITY
- Evidence is thin. One substantive negative on kitchensrated: a two-year unresolved claim over peeling finish, attributed by the company to moisture, with the owner's painters blaming the undercoat, and no site inspection performed.
- Single-review sourcing is not a pattern. **Flag as insufficient service evidence** rather than scoring it down.

## TIER FLAGS FOR SCORER
- Genuine custom shop, dealer-channel, mid-to-high-end clientele. Above semi-custom, below the ultra-luxury European tier.
- The company's own framing (best VALUE in high-end custom) is consistent with the Tom Miller allocation thesis: real quality, not trophy spend
- Dovetail box construction is a checkable claim — the walk cabinet forensics (boxes, glides, interiors) either confirms it or doesn't
