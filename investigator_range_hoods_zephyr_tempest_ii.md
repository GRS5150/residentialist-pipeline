### Company Background
Zephyr Ventilation is a privately held company headquartered in San Francisco, California [SRC-008]. The company designs products in San Francisco but manufactures through partnership with Chinese OEM facilities [SRC-001, SRC-008]. Zephyr positions itself as a design-forward alternative to traditional ventilation brands, focusing on balancing aesthetics with performance [SRC-008]. The company operates in the mid-premium segment, offering modern design at lower price points than Wolf or Vent-A-Hood [SRC-003]. Financial health and ownership structure details are not publicly disclosed as a private company.

### Strengths
- **External Blower Performance**: BLB series external blower delivers up to 1200 CFM with dramatically reduced noise, creating near-silent kitchen operation [SRC-001, SRC-007]
- **Designer Adoption**: Appeared 26 times in luxury real estate listings alongside Wolf and Vent-A-Hood, indicating strong professional specification [SRC-002]

### Deficiencies
- **Construction Quality**: China manufacturing results in thinner body gauge and good-but-not-premium build quality compared to Vent-A-Hood or Wolf [SRC-003]
- **Service Infrastructure**: Parts not widely stocked at independent distributors and customer service can be slow with non-streamlined ordering compared to Broan or Wolf [SRC-005, SRC-007]

### What You Should Know
The Tempest II is Zephyr's sweet spot product that balances modern design with solid performance. Kitchen designers specify it for clients who want contemporary aesthetics without Wolf pricing. The real value proposition emerges with the external blower option, which transforms the hood from moderate performer to high-performance, near-silent unit — though this adds $500-1200 to installation costs.

The trade-off is construction quality. While the stainless steel body and baffle filters are well-executed, the overall build doesn't match premium competitors. The motor is adequate but will wear faster than premium alternatives. If you choose the external blower configuration, this becomes less relevant since the motor moves outside the hood.
