### Company Background
KraftMaid is owned by Cabinetworks Group Michigan LLC, which was acquired by private equity firm Platinum Equity in May 2021. The company has faced significant financial stress, with Q4 2021 EBITDA falling 50% year-over-year due to raw material inflation, and a failed 2023 refinancing that required Platinum to commit $400M in additional equity [SRC-010]. This financial pressure manifested in three major plant closures during 2024, including facilities in Marshall and Jefferson, Texas, and Thompsontown, Pennsylvania, eliminating 770+ jobs and reducing operations from 14-15 facilities to 13 [SRC-009]. KraftMaid resigned from KCMA certification in October 2022 over disputes regarding anti-circumvention enforcement, though the company states products still meet A161.1 standards [SRC-008].

### Strengths
- **Hardware Quality**: Premium Blum components throughout with Whisper Touch SureMount hinges tested to 200,000 cycles and undermount slides rated for 90 lbs/100,000 cycles [SRC-003]
- **Drawer Construction**: Solid 3/4" hardwood dovetail construction on all four corners with captured plywood bottoms [SRC-003]

### Deficiencies
- **Standard Box Construction**: Ships with particleboard boxes rather than plywood, requiring expensive upgrades to match construction quality of lower-priced competitors like Fabuwood [SRC-001, SRC-002]
- **Warranty Limitations**: Lifetime warranty excludes labor costs for reinstallation, making even successful warranty claims cost-prohibitive for homeowners [SRC-012, SRC-006]

### What You Should Know
KraftMaid represents a classic "strong hardware on weak boxes" value proposition. You're paying for genuinely premium Blum soft-close hardware and solid wood drawer construction, but the standard configuration uses particleboard cabinet boxes that professionals consistently identify as the product's primary weakness. Multiple sources confirm you must upgrade both KraftMaid and competitor Integrity to plywood construction to match the standard construction quality of less expensive imported brands.

The company's financial situation adds risk to your purchase decision. Parent company Cabinetworks Group has closed three manufacturing facilities in 2024 alone, and the private equity ownership structure with documented EBITDA compression suggests ongoing operational challenges. While the lifetime warranty sounds appealing, the exclusion of labor costs means even successful claims require you to pay reinstallation costs that often exceed cabinet replacement value.

### Corporate Outlook
**Concern** — Three plant closures in 2024, failed refinancing requiring $400M PE bailout, and EBITDA compression indicate operational stress that could affect long-term support and availability.

### Repair Economics
Most common failure is particleboard moisture damage around sink/dishwasher areas (5-10 year timeframe). Box replacement typically requires complete cabinet replacement ($800-1500+ per unit) making repair uneconomical. Paint peeling at cope-and-stick joints is secondary issue but industry-wide problem for painted wood doors.

### Score Justification
The 80/100 score reflects KraftMaid's bifurcated quality profile — genuinely premium hardware and drawer construction earning high durability and performance marks, offset by standard particleboard box construction that compromises overall quality ratings. The company's financial stress and poor consumer satisfaction surveys prevent higher scoring despite excellent individual components.
