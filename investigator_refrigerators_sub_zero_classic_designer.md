### What You Should Know
Sub-Zero represents the gold standard in built-in refrigeration, backed by an unprecedented 38-year zero DOA record that no competitor can match. The dual-compressor architecture provides independent cooling zones that eliminate cross-contamination of failure modes while delivering NASA-grade air purification that measurably extends produce freshness. Models from 2023+ feature upgraded Split Climate™ variable-speed compressor technology on Classic and Pro lines, while legacy 700 and BI series retain proven fixed-speed Embraco compressors.

The company's parts commitment since 1986 and factory-certified technician network ensure long-term serviceability that justifies the premium price. Multiple owners report 15-25+ year service life with minimal repairs, making the initial investment economically rational over the appliance's lifetime. While the ice maker remains a common weak point across the industry, Sub-Zero's modular design allows for easier replacement without major service calls.
