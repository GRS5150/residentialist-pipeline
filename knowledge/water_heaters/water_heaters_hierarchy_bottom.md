### Reliability Data and Pro Opinions
- **Rheem Performance Plus**: Efficient hot water, easy install, ample supply per users; enhanced insulation/recovery vs. base Performance. Some older Rheem models outlast newer retail ones.[1][5][7][8]
- **A.O. Smith Signature**: Best overall electric/hybrid in tests; scale prevention in tankless. Reliable but retail-grade vs. pro lines.[6]
- **GE/Haier, Whirlpool, Kenmore, State/American**: Sparse pro data; rebadged from majors, so middling reliability. State excels in specifics like condensing tanks.[6]
- **Budget Options**: EcoSmart/Camplux tankless prone to scaling/inconsistency; generics lack anode protection, leading to tank rust. Stiebel Accelera ranks well in heat pumps but budget electrics draw complaints.[2]

- No brand universally "warned against," but plumbers push Professional/Platinum over retail for "quality homes."[3]
