## WHAT CHANGED AND WHY

The Andersen A-Series DH was initially evaluated with a 2B Materials Durability score of **7.31** — which the Challenge Bot flagged and the Council confirmed as invalid.

**The problem:** Fibrex composite carries a rubric base score of **6** (Composite/proprietary tier). The maximum achievable adjusted score for this material class is **base 6 + 1 (published composition/longevity data) = 7**. A net 2B of 7.31 cannot be reproduced from documented inputs under any valid reading of the rubric.

The Technical Purist relabeled this from "hierarchy violation" to "documentation gap" — but the correction direction is the same: the score must come down.

**Glazing bead confirmation:** Per the Andersen help center (confirmed during Council audit), the A-Series DH glazing bead is chemically bonded and cosmetic only. IGU replacement requires full sash replacement, not glass-only swap. This is a documented serviceability limitation that belongs in the 2B calculation.
