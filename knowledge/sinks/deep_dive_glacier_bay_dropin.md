### Company Background
Glacier Bay is Home Depot's exclusive private-label brand with no manufacturing capabilities of its own. Products are manufactured by various OEMs, with sinks and faucets suspected to be produced by Globe Union/Foremost under contract. The brand exists solely as Home Depot's entry-level positioning with no plumbing supply house distribution or professional installer endorsement. As a private label, there's no independent corporate entity behind Glacier Bay — buyers are relying on Home Depot's retail commitment to the brand rather than a dedicated plumbing manufacturer's reputation and expertise.

### What You Should Know
This sink represents the builder-grade minimum standard found in entry-level new construction. The 22-gauge stainless steel construction is the thinnest commonly available, resulting in a sink that physically flexes when you push on it and amplifies every sound. Professional plumbers consistently identify this as generating the most callbacks and replacements in their experience.

The 1-year warranty stands in stark contrast to lifetime warranties offered by established sink manufacturers, signaling minimal manufacturer confidence in longevity. Users report permanent water staining, denting, and mounting hardware degradation within 3 years of normal kitchen use. The included faucet is universally dismissed as disposable-grade by trade professionals.
