### Pure Genius Air-Purifying Technology [CRITICAL]

**What is Pure Genius?**

Pure Genius is a **titanium dioxide (TiO₂) photocatalytic technology** embedded in the finish or core that is claimed to:
- Break down formaldehyde and VOCs via photocatalytic oxidation when exposed to ambient light (natural or artificial UV)
- React with oxygen and water vapor to convert formaldehyde into CO₂ and H₂O

**Does Pure Genius actually work?**

This is a **contentious claim in the hardwood industry.** Here's the evidence landscape:

**Pro claims:**
- Titanium dioxide is a proven photocatalyst in air purification literature (academic journals confirm TiO₂ efficacy in controlled lab settings)
- Lauzon has conducted internal testing (not independently published)

**Skepticism from independent sources:**
- **No peer-reviewed independent testing published** in hardwood flooring or indoor air quality journals specific to Lauzon Pure Genius
- **Photocatalytic efficiency under real-world residential conditions is questionable:**
  - Efficacy requires direct UV light exposure; indoor ambient light may be insufficient for meaningful VOC reduction
  - Formaldehyde off-gassing occurs primarily during first 3–6 months post-installation; continuous photocatalysis would need to operate during this window
  - Air circulation patterns in rooms mean formaldehyde molecules must contact the floor surface—not guaranteed
- **r/HardwoodFloors consensus:** Installers and consumers largely view Pure Genius as a **marketing differentiator with unproven real-world impact**. No widespread reports of homes with Lauzon Pure Genius having superior indoor air quality vs. non-Pure Genius products.

**Duration of air-purifying effect:**
- Titanium dioxide is photocatalytically active indefinitely if not damaged, but finish wear over 5–10 years reduces photocatalytic surface area.
- Practical efficacy period: **theoretical lifetime, but diminishing returns with wear.**

**Professional consensus:** Pure Genius is a **"nice-to-have" marketing claim with limited independent validation.** If formaldehyde reduction is your primary concern, prioritize **CARB Phase 2 compliance and low adhesive formaldehyde content** (see Material Safety below) rather than relying on Pure Genius photocatalysis.
