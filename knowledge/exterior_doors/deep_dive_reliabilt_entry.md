### Company Background
Reliabilt is Lowe's exclusive house brand for doors and windows, manufactured through various supplier partnerships primarily in Asia and North America. The brand represents Lowe's strategy to offer competitively-priced building products with retailer control over specifications and pricing. As a house brand, Reliabilt products are manufactured to hit specific retail price points rather than optimize performance, with manufacturing contracts that can shift between suppliers based on cost considerations. The brand is backed by Lowe's financial stability but represents the value-tier of their door offerings rather than premium construction.

### Strengths
- **Enhanced Security Features**: Multi-point locking system as standard provides three-point engagement at top, middle, and bottom of frame, representing significant security improvement over basic single-point deadbolt configurations [src_008]

### Deficiencies
- **Structural Construction Limitations**: Bonded-frame construction uses adhesive bonding between steel edge and door skin panels, creating documented failure modes under impact loading where bonds can delaminate, particularly in high-stress areas like lower hinges [src_002, src_006]
- **Quality Control Inconsistency**: Field reports document variable weatherstripping installation and seal quality across units, requiring professional installers to add supplemental weatherstripping to achieve acceptable performance [src_004, src_005]

### What You Should Know
This is a budget-tier entry door designed to meet basic residential requirements at retail price points. The construction approach prioritizes cost savings through bonded-frame assembly rather than welded construction, which creates potential long-term durability concerns under heavy use or impact. Professional installers consistently report needing to supplement the factory weathersealing to achieve adequate air infiltration performance.

The multi-point locking system is a genuine security upgrade that adds value, but the overall construction reflects compromises made to hit aggressive price targets. If you're looking for a temporary solution or have budget constraints, this door can provide basic functionality, but expect to potentially address weathersealing issues during installation and possible structural concerns over time.

### Platform Disclosure

### Repair Economics
Most common failures involve weatherstripping replacement ($25-50 in materials) and adhesive bond separation requiring professional repair ($200-400). Given the door's price point, major structural repairs often exceed cost-justification for replacement.
