## Primary Research Strategy (Not Secondary Analysis)

### **DOOR CORE & SLAB CONSTRUCTION**

**Fiberglass Skins:**
- **Direct contact**: Call Therma-Tru, Masonite, JELD-WEN, Plastpro sales teams asking: "Do you manufacture your own fiberglass skins or source from X? Do you supply other brands?"
- **Why this matters**: Some manufacturers do custom-mold for OEMs; others are white-label suppliers. This is rarely published but sales teams will confirm.
- **Verification**: Check SEC filings (Therma-Tru parent Fortune Brands, Masonite public) for manufacturing capacity disclosures.

**Steel Door Gauge & Foam Spec:**
- **Source**: Product spec sheets (downloadable PDFs from brand websites) — compare 5-10 models across price tiers
- **Building Science Forum**: GreenBuildingAdvisor.com has threads where contractors compare door performance; search "steel door 22 vs 24 gauge"
- **Test reports**: Manufacturers often publish NFRC or ANSI test data — request these directly

**Wood Door Manufacturers:**
- Simpson Door Company, TruStile, JELD-WEN IWP, Trustile — these are well-known
- **Gap to fill**: Are there regional or custom mills? Check FHB (Fine Homebuilding) archives for articles on semi-custom wood doors
- **Wood species durability**: USDA Forest Products Lab has decay resistance data; this is scientific and verifiable

**Iron/Steel Decorative Doors:**
- These are often semi-custom fabricators, not mass manufacturers
- **Research path**: Visit their websites, call for spec sheets — most will discuss gauge, welding (MIG vs TIG), and hardware sourcing
- **Red flag**: If they won't discuss specifications, it's likely commodity assembly
