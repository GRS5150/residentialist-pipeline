### Professional Consensus on Exterior Door Quality Tiers

Professionals (installers, contractors, and builders) draw the line between **good** residential exterior entry doors (durable 15-25+ years with minimal callbacks, suitable for quality homes) and **mediocre** (builder-grade, prone to failure in 5-10 years) primarily at **mid-tier fiberglass/steel from reputable brands like Therma-Tru Classic-Craft or Masonite Performance**, emphasizing slab construction, weatherstrip longevity, and U-factor performance over aesthetics.[2][4][5] Below this: high callback rates from warping, water intrusion, and finish failure in big-box steel/fiberglass; pros refuse Reliabilt, American Craftsman, and discontinued Feather River for production homes.[4][5]

#### Specifics by Material
- **Steel Doors**: **20-gauge or thicker** separates real from commodity (16-18 best). Commodity = 24-26 gauge (warps in heat). Good makers: Masonite Performance, JELD-WEN Premium (if dealer-grade). Therma-Tru Smooth-Star is **floor** (22-gauge typical, acceptable but warps faster than fiberglass).[2][5]
- **Fiberglass Doors**: Therma-Tru Benchmark **worse than Classic-Craft** (thinner compression molding, less realistic grain, higher delam risk). Masonite matches Benchmark but excels in seals.[4][5][6]
- **Builder-Grade Wood**: None "good enough"—all prone to rot/swelling without premium cladding (e.g., Simpson solid wood). Wood = **premium only** ($3k+).[6]
- **Big-Box vs. Others**: Reliabilt/American Craftsman/Steves **not identical**—Reliabilt worst (thinnest), Steves mid (better hinges). All Tier 4.[1][4]

#### Channel Differences (Key: Big-Box vs. Dealer)
Yes—**same brand, different products**. Therma-Tru/Masonite/JELD-WEN ship **downgraded lines** to Home Depot/Lowe's (thinner steel, basic weatherstrip, no premium hinges) vs. dealer showrooms (full-spec, prehung options). Pros confirm: "Big-box gets builder slop."[4][5]

#### Pella Doors
**Mid-tier overall** (Tier 2-3), not matching window hierarchy. Reserve = good fiberglass (like Therma-Tru mid); 250 Series = builder (avoid). Pros rank below ProVia/Therma-Tru Classic-Craft, above JELD-WEN Builders. Strong in seals/U-factor (R6+).[2][6]

**Floor of acceptable**: Therma-Tru Benchmark/Smooth-Star or Masonite Performance from dealers—pros install these in $500k+ homes without hesitation. Below: Big-box only for flips/rentals.[4][5][6]
