Professionals draw the line between **good residential faucets** (reliable 10+ years with brass construction, ceramic/quality cartridges like DST or Kerox, NSF/ANSI 61 certification, and durable finishes) and **mediocre/builder-grade** (ZAMAK/pot metal bodies, plastic internals, failing in 3-5 years from cartridge leaks or finish rust) based on material durability, cartridge cycle life (e.g., 500,000+ switches), lead-free compliance, and parts availability.[1][3][4][7]
