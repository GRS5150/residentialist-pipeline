### What Separates the Best from Excellent
- **Top Tier (Dekton, Cambria, Caesarstone)**: Non-porous engineering ensures **zero sealing**, superior **lot-to-lot consistency** (critical for fabricators), and lab-proven metrics like flexural strength (Dekton leads) and stain resistance (quartz edges out). Warranties execute reliably without disclaimers voiding for "normal wear." Long-term: No etching/fading in high-use scenarios.[1][3]
- **Excellent but Variable (Naturals)**: Premium slabs shine aesthetically and in heat tolerance but demand **annual sealing**, show **inherent variation** (e.g., quartzite porosity), and risk etching/cracking. Fabricators note more callbacks for repairs.[2][6]
- **Pro Insights**: Kitchen experts rank quartz #1 for busy homes due to resin-enhanced predictability; Dekton/porcelain for extremes (heat/UV outdoors). Naturals for those accepting maintenance trade-offs.[2][3][5][8]
