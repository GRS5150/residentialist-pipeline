# Residential Windows Testing & Measurement Framework

## 1. Standardized Tests: What Actually Gets Measured

### AAMA/WDMA/CSA 101-I.S.2-A440 (North American Fenestration Standard)
**What it measures:**
- Air infiltration (ASTM E283): Measured in cfm/sq ft at 0.3" water column pressure. Pass/fail threshold: ≤0.30 cfm/sq ft for residential. *Numeric spread in market: 0.05–0.25 cfm/sq ft differentiates premium from builder-grade.*
- Water penetration resistance (ASTM E331): Spray test at 2% of positive pressure design load. Pass/fail only—not a continuous metric.
- Structural wind load (ASTM E330): Tests at design pressure (DP) rating. Yields DP number (15–80+ for residential).
- Thermal cycling, impact safety, forced-entry resistance per regional codes.

**Who certifies:** AAMA (American Architectural Manufacturers Association) maintains the standard. Third-party labs conduct testing; certification is valid when tested to this standard.

**DP Rating System (critical for differentiation):**
- DP number = pressure in psf the window resists without failure
- DP 15–30: Standard residential (suburban conditions)
- DP 40–50: High-wind zones (coastal, tornado regions)
- DP 60+: Extreme conditions (Miami-Dade, hurricane zones)
- *Not a pass/fail but a continuous performance metric*—real differentiation exists

---

### NFRC 100/200/500 (Fenestration Performance Rating Council)
**What it measures:**
- **U-Factor** (thermal transmittance): BTU/hr·sq ft·°F. Lower = better insulation. 
  - *Numeric spread: 0.22–0.55 across residential market*
  - Premium (triple-pane, krypton): 0.22–0.28
  - Mid-range (dual-pane, Low-E): 0.28–0.35
  - Budget (dual-pane, standard): 0.45–0.55

- **SHGC** (Solar Heat Gain Coefficient): 0–1 scale. How much solar heat enters.
  - *Numeric spread: 0.18–0.40 across market*
  - Premium: 0.18–0.24 (high control)
  - Budget: 0.30–0.40

- **VT** (Visible Transmittance): How much light enters (0–1 scale).
  - *Numeric spread: 0.50–0.70*
  - Premium: 0.65–0.70 (minimal haze from coatings)
  - Budget: 0.50–0.60

- **CRF** (Condensation Resistance Factor, NFRC 500): 0–100 scale. Predicts condensation formation.
  - *Numeric spread: 35–75*
  - Premium: 65–75
  - Budget: 35–50

**Who certifies:** NFRC is independent, nonprofit. Labs accredited by NFRC conduct testing. Manufacturers can submit windows for optional certification; results published in NFRC Directory.

---

### ASTM E283 (Air Infiltration)
- Pressure difference method at 0.3" water column
- Measured in CFM/sq ft of operable window area
- *Citable numeric spread: 0.05–0.25 CFM/sq ft for residential*
- Not all windows tested; premium brands typically publish, budget brands often don't

---

### ASTM E331 (Water Penetration)
- Spray test; pass/fail only
- No numeric differentiation in public data
- All compliant windows essentially equivalent on this metric

---

### ASTM E330 (Structural Wind Load)
- Yields DP rating (see above)
- *Critical differentiator: DP 15 vs DP 50*

---

### ASTM E547 (Cyclic Pressure Water Penetration)
- Pass/fail; no public numeric spread data
- Tests durability under repeated wet/pressure cycles
- Rarely published independently

---

### ASTM E1886/E1996 (Impact Resistance)
- Missile impact testing (simulates hurricane debris)
- Pass/fail only
- Required in hurricane zones; most premium brands publish

---

### AAMA 910 (Voluntary Lifecycle Specification)
- Specifies product longevity, aging, maintenance cycles
- Very few manufacturers certify to this
- Pass/fail structure; not a continuous metric
- **Source: AAMA publishes certified product lists**

### Corner Joint Method

| Joint Type | Strength | Cost | Typical Brands |

| **Fusion-Welded** (vinyl/fiberglass heated and joined) | Strongest (~90% of material strength) | $$$ | Marvin, Loewen, Kolbe |
