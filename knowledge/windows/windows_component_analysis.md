## WHAT I CAN VERIFY (PUBLIC DATA)

### **Glass Packages (IGU)**

**Cardinal Industries Supply:**
- Cardinal is the dominant IGU supplier to Marvin, Andersen, JELD-WEN, Pella, Milgard, and most mid-to-premium brands
- LoE coatings: LoE-272 (single-silver, standard efficiency), LoE-366 (triple-silver, premium). These are published specs.
- Most brands do **not** let consumers specify coating—it's bundled by package tier
- Triple-pane: Marvin offers standard (not optional), Andersen partial, JELD-WEN optional, Pella limited

**Gas Retention:**
- Argon: published ~1% annual loss (Vitro/Cardinal data sheets)
- Krypton/Xenon: proprietary retention curves, rarely published
- **Gap:** Actual field data (20-year cohort) is held by manufacturers and insurers, not public

**Spacer Technology:**
- Intercept (PPG): stainless steel, thermal break design
- Super Spacer (Quanex): foam-filled (proprietary foam spec)
- TPS (Thermoplastic Spacer): proprietary resin
- **Known:**  Foam-filled spacers show better thermal performance; seal failure data by brand is **NOT published**
- **You'll need:** Contractor + home inspector surveys, NFRC database analysis, claims data from window warranty administrators

**Glass Thickness Default:**
- North American standard: 3mm (1/8"), tempered in safety zones (per model/code)
- High-end: some brands offer 4mm option
- **Source:** NFRC testing, building code tables, manufacturer spec sheets (publicly available)

### **Frame Materials & Construction**

**Wood Interior Species (by brand — publicly documented):**
- **Marvin:** Scandinavian pine (standard), Douglas fir (premium)
- **Andersen:** Ponderosa pine (standard), hardwoods in premium lines
- **Pella:** Pine (standard), alder/oak in specialty
- **JELD-WEN:** Mixed (varies by line)
- **Milgard:** Mainly pine
- **Source:** Manufacturer spec sheets, installer guides

**Exterior Cladding:**
- **Extruded Aluminum:** Marvin (Ultimate), Andersen (100-series+)
- **Fiberglass:** Marvin Ultrex, Pella Impervia, Milgard (polyester resin, proprietary formulations—exact resin chemistry is proprietary)
- **Vinyl:** JELD-WEN, Ply Gem, Milgard, Semco
- **PVC:** Rare in North America
- **Cladding thickness:** Published in spec sheets (typically 0.040"–0.080" aluminum, fiberglass pultruded as single-wall ~0.25")

**Corner Joint Construction:**
- **Mortise-and-tenon:** Traditional (Marvin, Loewen)
- **Fusion-welded:** Vinyl brands (JELD-WEN, Milgard, Ply Gem)
- **Mitered/bonded:** Fiberglass (Pella Impervia, Marvin Ultrex)
- **Longevity comparisons:** NOT published by manufacturers; requires long-term field studies

**Known Failure Modes (documented in installer/contractor forums):**
- Vinyl warping: dark surfaces + heat (real, thermal expansion)
- Wood rot behind cladding: water penetration + poor drainage design
- Fiberglass cracking: stress concentration at corner joints, documented in early Pella Impervia units
- Aluminum thermal bridging: known physics, quantifiable via U-factor
