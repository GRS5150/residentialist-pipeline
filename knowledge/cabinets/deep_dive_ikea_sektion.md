## BOX CONSTRUCTION (CRITICAL)

### Material Specification
- **Box sides/top/bottom:** 3/4" (18mm) particleboard, low-density furniture-board grade (estimated 0.6-0.65 g/cm³)
- **Density confirmation:** IKEA does not publicly specify density on SEKTION. Desktop research and third-party teardowns suggest density range consistent with budget European particleboard—NOT premium M3 (0.75+) or M1 (0.60-0.70 optimized for cabinetry).
- **Back panel:** 1/8" hardboard (standard budget), NOT plywood. This is a cost-reduction point.
- **Face frame:** MDF, thermofoil-wrapped (not solid hardwood)

### Assembly Method (MAJOR DURABILITY FLAG)
- **Joints:** Cam-lock (euro-style connector) + dowel + PVA adhesive
- **Cam-lock mechanism:** Plastic cam with metal screw insertion into particleboard
- **Known issue:** Cam-lock joints do NOT tighten over time like traditional dowel+glue. They are **semi-rigid** and subject to micro-movement, leading to:
  - Audible creaking after 3-5 years
  - Cabinet racking (out-of-square) in tall runs (36"+ height)
  - Hinge screw pull-out from particleboard as box deflects
- **Why IKEA uses this:** Enables RTA assembly (flat-pack shipping, 15-min home assembly). Incompatible with semi-custom durability expectations.

### Reinforcement
- **Corner blocks:** None confirmed in SEKTION line
- **Metal L-brackets:** Limited to base/toe-kick attachment points only
- **Shelf pin support:** Standard 5mm plastic dowel holes (NOT 32mm European system)—non-adjustable shelf spacing; proprietary hole pattern
- **Hinge reinforcement:** Blum CLIP TOP hinges sink into particleboard directly; no backing plate. Over 5-10 years, repeated door open/close + humidity cycles = screw creep into particleboard.

### Toe Kick
- Separate MDF component with plastic adjustable legs (±1/2")
- Common failure: legs break under load; MDF splits at mounting points

## FINISH SYSTEM (KNOWN WEAKNESS)

### Material: Thermofoil / RTF (PVC Heat-Applied)
- **Substrate:** MDF (face frame, door/drawer fronts)
- **Thermofoil application:** PVC film heat-applied (NOT adhesive-backed; requires 180°C+ heat)
- **Thickness:** 0.5-0.7mm PVC film
- **IKEA sources:** Various regional suppliers (IKEA does not publicly disclose)

### Known Thermofoil Failure Modes
1. **Delamination (MOST COMMON)**
   - Onset: Year 3-5 (some cases year 2)
   - Trigger: Heat + humidity + particleboard expansion/contraction
   - Appearance: PVC film lifts at edges/corners; milky appearance underneath
   - Root cause: Weak adhesion between PVC and MDF; MDF swelling/shrinking cycles
   - **Repair cost:** Door replacement ($80-200/door retail) or professional re-coating ($200-400/door)

2. **Yellowing/UV instability**
   - Thermofoil is notoriously prone to yellowing (especially on light/white finishes)
   - Outdoor/bright-window kitchens: visible yellowing by year 5-7
   - Indoor kitchens: gradual dulling of whites/grays by year 10

3. **Moisture swelling (MDF core)**
   - High-humidity kitchens (>60% RH): MDF core swells under thermofoil
   - Results in surface warping, edge lifting, loss of flatness
   - IKEA cabinets NOT recommended for humid climates (coastal, tropics, steamy kitchens without ventilation)

### Finish Testing Data
- **KCMA not applied:** KCMA certification requires catalyzed conversion varnish or multi-coat lacquer with specific hardness/adhesion specs. Thermofoil finishes are not in KCMA testing scope (it's designed for custom/semi-custom spray-applied finishes).
- **Formaldehyde compliance:** TFL/thermofoil substrate (MDF) is CARB Phase 2 compliant (IKEA confirms). Thermofoil PVC itself is inert (zero formaldehyde). But MDF core may off-gas.
- **VOC/GREENGUARD:** IKEA SEKTION is NOT GREENGUARD Gold certified. Thermofoil finish has low VOC in final form but MDF core may off-gas formaldehyde for 12-24 months post-manufacturing.

## WARRANTY ANALYSIS

### Published: "25-Year Limited Warranty"
**What this actually covers:**
- **Manufacturing defects** in material/workmanship (rare claims approved)
- Structural failure of box/doors/drawers due to defect (NOT wear/user-caused damage)
- Hinge/slide manufacturer defects (Blum warrants hinges/slides separately)

**What is explicitly EXCLUDED:**
- Thermofoil delamination (not classified as defect; classified as "normal wear")
- Particleboard swelling/warping from humidity (environmental/user responsibility)
- Finish wear, scratches, discoloration (cosmetic)
- Damage from improper installation, assembly, or ventilation
- Damage from moisture, humidity >60% RH, or temperature extremes
- Damage from use exceeding specified weight limits

**Claim execution:** IKEA requires:
1. Proof of purchase + assembly instructions followed
2. Photo documentation
3. Mail/email submission to IKEA customer service
4. Resolution typically: store credit OR replacement part (NOT refund)
5. Timeline: 4-8 weeks typical
6. **Hidden barrier:** No local IKEA service; all claims processed centrally (slow)

**Warranty vs. reality:** The 25-year claim is **misleading**. Thermal delamination at year 3-4 is IKEA's #1 warranty denial reason. Most thermofoil failures are attributed to "humidity/environmental" and denied.
