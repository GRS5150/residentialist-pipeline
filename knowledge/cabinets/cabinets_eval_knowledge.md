## SCORING LANE BOUNDARIES

### What We Score (Construction Quality & Durability)

**Box/Carcass Construction:**
- Substrate material: plywood, industrial-grade particleboard (45#), standard particleboard (15#), MDF
- Substrate thickness: sides (1/2" framed standard, 3/4" frameless standard), back (1/4" minimum, 1/2" better, 3/4" best), bottom, top
- Assembly method: dowel + glue, cam lock, stapled, pocket screw
- Shelf material and thickness (adjustable vs fixed, edge banding)
- Interior finish type: melamine, UV-coated veneer, raw, thermofused laminate

**Drawer System (The "Cartridge" of Cabinets — Highest-Signal Component):**
- Drawer box material: solid hardwood dovetail, plywood, stapled particleboard, metal (Legrabox/Vionaro)
- Drawer box joinery: dovetail (gold standard), dowel, stapled, lock-rabbet
- Slide/glide manufacturer: Blum, Grass, Hettich, Salice, KV, Accuride, DTC, unbranded
- Slide type: undermount (premium), side-mount (standard), center-mount (cheap)
- Slide features: full-extension, soft-close, weight rating (typically 75-150 lbs)
- **Slide is the single highest-signal hardware component.** Blum Tandem/Movento = gold standard. Grass DynaPro = excellent. Hettich = good value. DTC/unbranded = builder-grade flag.

**Door & Hinge System:**
- Door construction: solid wood (5-piece raised/recessed panel), MDF slab, thermofoil (vinyl-wrapped MDF), wood-veneer MDF
- Hinge manufacturer: Blum, Grass, Hettich, Salice — same tier hierarchy as slides
- Hinge type: concealed cup (European), face-mount (dated/cheap)
- Hinge features: soft-close, 6-way adjustability, tool-less clip mounting
- Door cycles tested: KCMA requires 25,000 open/close cycles

**Finish System:**
- Stain finish: catalyzed conversion varnish (best durability), lacquer, polyurethane
- Paint finish: catalyzed conversion varnish topcoat (oven-cured preferred), sprayed lacquer, brushed
- Thermofoil: vinyl film heat-applied to MDF — cheapest, poorest longevity, delaminates with heat/moisture
- UV-cured topcoat: used on interior surfaces for durability

**Construction Type:**
- Framed (face frame): Traditional North American. Solid wood frame attached to front of box. Door attaches to frame.
- Frameless (European/full-access): No face frame. Door attaches directly to box sides. Wider opening, more accessible storage. Requires 3/4" sides for rigidity.

### What We Present But Do Not Score
- Cabinet dimensions and sizing options
- Number of door styles and finish colors available
- Lead time / delivery timeline
- RTA (ready-to-assemble) vs pre-assembled vs custom
- Price per linear foot (reported as tier, not exact — prices vary by dealer)

### What We Do Not Touch
- Kitchen layout or design recommendations
- Countertop pairing suggestions
- Installation methods or contractor recommendations
- Color/style aesthetic judgments
- Health/toxicity concerns (evaluated separately by Material Safety Bot)

**Principle:** A cabinet is a manufactured product with a box, hardware, finish, and measurable construction quality. We evaluate the manufactured product. We do not design kitchens. We do not evaluate health — that's a separate axis with a separate bot.

## SOURCE MAP

### Tier 1 — Primary Intelligence Sources

**Main Line Kitchen Design / Paul McAlary (mainlinekitchendesign.com)**
Closest thing to "StarCraft for cabinets." Kitchen designer and dealer in Philadelphia. 157+ brands rated with construction-specific criteria (back thickness, plywood vs particleboard, hardware brand, assembly method). Rates on both construction quality AND value for price tier. Updates annually. Calls out specific construction details other sources ignore (1/4" backs, hanging rail thickness, melamine vs plywood upgrades). Also a dealer for several brands — conflict of interest exists but methodology is most transparent available.

**WOODWEB Cabinetmaking Forum (woodweb.com)**
Professional cabinetmaker forum. Component-level discussions: hinge performance over 5+ years, slide comparison (Blum vs Grass vs Hettich real-world), drawer box construction debate, finish system longevity. Working professionals comparing notes. Must be mined thread by thread.

**Fine Woodworking Forum (finewoodworking.com/forum)**
Professional and advanced amateur woodworkers discussing hardware quality, construction methods, material selection. Older threads (2007-2015) particularly valuable for long-term performance data.

### Tier 2 — Certification & Standards

**KCMA / ANSI A161.1 Certification**
Only performance standard for kitchen and bath cabinets. 60+ years. Tests: structural integrity (600 lb wall load, 15 psf shelf load held 7 days), drawer operation (25,000 cycles), door operation (25,000 swing cycles, 65 lb weighted door test), finish quality (detergent exposure, hot/cold cycling). Baseline pass/fail gate. Absence in production brands IS a flag. Absence in small custom brands may not be.

### Tier 3 — Consumer/Community Sources

**Houzz Forums** — Homeowner discussions comparing brands. Useful for real-world experience but unstructured.
**Reddit (r/HomeImprovement, r/KitchenDesign)** — Occasionally valuable when professionals contribute.
**Consumer Reports** — Tests limited brands on surface-level function. Does NOT evaluate substrate material, hardware manufacturer, or assembly method. Essentially useless for our methodology.

### Tier 4 — Component Manufacturers

**Drawer Slide Manufacturers:**
- **Blum** (Austria/USA): Gold standard. Tandem/Movento undermount slides. Integrated soft-close. Highest precision, broadest adjustment. Lifetime warranty. Used by KraftMaid, Crystal, premium lines.
- **Grass** (Germany): DynaPro slides excellent — 40kg rating vs Blum Tandem 30kg. Tiomos hinges innovative. Strong in European frameless.
- **Hettich** (Germany): Good quality, lower price than Blum. Used by Crystal mid-tier lines, some production brands. Reliable, slightly less refined.
- **Salice** (Italy, founded 1924): Strong hinges. Silentia+ soft-close good. Lifetime warranty. Cost savings vs Blum.
- **DTC** (China): Budget option. Found in imported/RTA lines. Functional but lower precision, shorter lifespan. Presence = data point.
- **KV / Knape & Vogt, Accuride**: Primarily side-mount slides. Not undermount players.

**Hinge Manufacturers:**
Same hierarchy as slides. Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION = gold standard. Grass Tiomos = innovative (damping in arm). Hettich = good value. Salice = solid mid-tier. DTC/unbranded = flag.

## FAILURE PATTERNS

### Particleboard Cabinets
- **Moisture failure** (#1): Under-sink leak = swelling and disintegration. Not repairable.
- **Screw pullout**: Hinge screws and shelf pins lose grip over time. Doors sag.
- **Edge delamination**: Melamine coating peels at edges, exposing substrate.
- **Timeline**: 5-7 years visible degradation in high-use. 15# fails faster than 45# industrial grade.

### MDF Components (Doors, Drawer Fronts)
- **Moisture sensitivity**: Worse than particleboard at edges. Swells, won't recover.
- **Thermofoil delamination**: Heat from ovens/dishwashers causes vinyl to separate from MDF. Cannot be repaired or refinished. Entire door replacement required. **Strongest "upgrade now" flag.**
- **Weight**: Heavier than plywood or solid wood equivalents.

### Plywood Cabinets
- **Potential warping**: Less common with quality plywood.
- **Cost**: ~20% premium over particleboard.
- **Longevity**: 20+ years. Moisture damage largely repairable.

### Thermofoil Doors
- **Delamination near heat sources**: Dishwasher steam, oven heat, sustained sunlight.
- **Cannot be repaired or refinished**: Must replace entire door.
- **Yellowing**: White thermofoil yellows with UV over time.
- **Single biggest "upgrade now" flag in production home spec sheet.**

### Painted Finish Cabinets
- All painted cabinets show wear. No brand immune.
- Catalyzed conversion varnish topcoat (oven-cured) is most durable.

## ALTERNATIVE RECOMMENDATION FRAMEWORK

### At or Below (Same or Lower Cost, Better Quality)
- Must be same form factor (framed↔framed, frameless↔frameless)
- Must be available through same or comparable supply chains in buyer's market
- Must be genuinely better on specific, named construction criteria
- Example: "Your builder spec'd Merillat Basics with particleboard boxes. Fabuwood Galaxy is comparable price, comes standard with all-plywood construction, dovetail drawers, and Blum-based soft-close hardware."

### Above (Budget Stretch for Quality Leap)
- Quantify the cost delta AND the specific quality jump
- Name exactly what changes: material, hardware manufacturer, construction method
- Flag the report context:
  - **Production:** "Upgrade now vs replace after closing" — cabinets are almost always an UPGRADE NOW item because replacing cabinets after closing is a full kitchen remodel
  - **Custom:** "Specify this instead" — client has full control, recommend the better option at each price tier

## CALIBRATION NOTES

1. **No single "StarCraft" source.** Main Line Kitchen Design closest but is also a dealer with brand loyalties. Must cross-reference with WOODWEB and direct manufacturer spec sheets.

2. **Hardware manufacturer is the highest-signal quality indicator.** Like cartridge manufacturer in faucets. Blum = quality commitment. DTC/undisclosed = cost optimization.

3. **"All plywood" is necessary but not sufficient.** Plywood quality varies. Always note plywood grade when available.

4. **Brand lines within same parent vary enormously.** KraftMaid base with particleboard is completely different from KraftMaid Vantage with plywood upgrade. MasterBrand's Aristokraft and Omega share parent but almost nothing in construction quality. Always evaluate the specific line/configuration, not just the brand name.

5. **The Texas production home cabinet is almost always Tier 1.** This is where we deliver most value — translating "your builder spec'd Timberlake Origins" into actionable upgrade decisions.
