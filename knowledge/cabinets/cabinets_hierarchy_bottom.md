### Professional Hierarchy of Residential Cabinet Brands

Professionals, including kitchen designers and installers, generally draw the line between **good** (semi-custom or upgraded stock with plywood boxes, soft-close hardware like Blum, and 10-20+ year durability) and **mediocre** (builder-grade with particleboard cores, basic glides, and 5-7 year failure risk) based on construction materials, hardware quality, and long-term reliability. Brands like **KraftMaid** and **Fabuwood** sit on or above this line for most pros, while **Hampton Bay**, **Diamond NOW**, and **RTA generics** mark the floor of acceptable quality—often tolerated only for flips or rentals but avoided for quality kitchens.[1][2][3]

#### Reliability Data and Known Problems
- **Top failures in cheap cabinets**: Drawer glides fail first (cheap metal bends/strips after 2-5 years heavy use); particleboard swells/delaminates from moisture (sinks, spills); thermofoil doors peel on humid cabinets. Pros report these in Hampton Bay, Diamond NOW, RTA after 5-7 years.[1]
