### Known Failure Modes (Professional Installer Reports)

From John Bridge Tile Forum archives and r/Tile feedback:

1. **Lippage (uneven edge heights):** Merola non-rectified tiles have high variance → professional installers use leveling systems (Raimondi, QEP).
2. **Chipping during cut:** Ceramic is more brittle than porcelain → edge chipping during wet saw cuts. Installers report higher scrap rates.

## INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS

### Substrate & Membrane
**Standard ceramic floor installation:**
- **Cement board or fully cured concrete slab** (ceramic can handle direct-to-slab if moisture-controlled).
- **Uncoupling membrane (Schluter DITRA) OPTIONAL but recommended** for crack prevention; significantly extends life.
- Professional installers: 80% use DITRA for ceramic floors. DIY: rarely.

### Thinset
**Modified thinset required** (ceramic has lower porosity than porcelain; unmodified may not bond adequately).
- Contractor standard: Mapei Keraflex, Laticrete 9235, similar polymer-modified formulas.

### Grout Line Minimum
**Merola non-rectified:** Minimum 9mm (3/8"), typical 12mm (1/2").
- Rectified porcelain: 1.5-3mm acceptable.
- Larger grout lines = higher visual tolerance for lippage + easier maintenance.
