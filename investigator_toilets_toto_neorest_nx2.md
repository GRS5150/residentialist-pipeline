### What You Should Know
The TOTO Neorest NX2 represents the gold standard in smart toilets, combining proven TORNADO FLUSH technology with advanced CeFiONtect nano-coating for superior performance and cleanliness. A 44-year veteran plumber's endorsement of "TOTO is the best period" reflects the professional consensus on build quality and reliability [A1]. The integrated design eliminates the modularity of separate Washlet seats, meaning smart feature repairs require TOTO-specific parts rather than third-party alternatives. Despite this limitation, TOTO's century-plus track record and vertical manufacturing integration provide the strongest backing available in the premium toilet market.

### Platform Disclosure
TOTO Neorest NX2 uses the same TORNADO FLUSH engine as the Ultramax II, Drake II, and other current-gen TOTO models. The bidet/Washlet module shares core technology with standalone TOTO Washlet seats (S7A, C5, etc.). Neorest integrates both into a single chassis, eliminating separate seat mounting but creating a non-modular replacement path for smart features.
