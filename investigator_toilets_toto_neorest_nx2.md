### Company Background
TOTO is a 107-year-old independent public company and global market leader in sanitary ware. Founded in Japan in 1917, TOTO maintains vertically integrated manufacturing from raw materials to finished products at their flagship Kitakyushu factory. The company dominates the Japanese market while experiencing strong growth in the US. As an established public company with over a century of operations, TOTO presents no going-concern financial risk and represents the premium tier of the toilet market rather than a repositioned value brand.

### What You Should Know
The Neorest NX2 represents the benchmark in smart toilet technology, combining TOTO's proven TORNADO FLUSH system with advanced bidet functionality in a single integrated unit. The CeFiONtect nano-coating technology provides superior long-term cleanliness, while the flush performance consistently achieves the highest independent testing standards.

However, the integrated design philosophy means electronic component failures become expensive proprietary repairs rather than simple seat replacements. This creates different ownership economics compared to mechanical toilets, where repair costs often exceed 50% of replacement value. For buyers committed to smart toilet technology, this represents the market's most proven platform backed by over a century of manufacturing expertise.

### Platform Disclosure
TOTO Neorest NX2 uses the same TORNADO FLUSH engine as the Ultramax II, Drake II, and other current-gen TOTO models. The bidet/Washlet module shares core technology with standalone TOTO Washlet seats (S7A, C5, etc.). Neorest integrates both into a single chassis, eliminating separate seat mounting but creating a non-modular replacement path for smart features.
