### Strengths
- **Dual-stacked sealed burner system**: Proprietary Wolf casting (not Sabaf OEM) delivers independent 20,000 BTU searing and 300-500 BTU precision simmer from same burner — benchmark 40:1 ratio for the category

### Deficiencies
- **Proprietary parts ecosystem**: Factory-certified service network required for repairs — no universal fit components increases long-term service costs
- **Sealed burner trade-off**: Slightly slower boil times versus open cast iron burners (BlueStar) due to sealed design, though cleaning advantages compensate

### What You Should Know
Wolf delivers the most refined gas range experience with dual-stacked burners that seamlessly transition from 20,000 BTU searing to 300 BTU simmering. The infrared broiler genuinely mimics commercial equipment. Build quality is exceptional with continuous cast iron grates and welded stainless construction. However, you're locked into Wolf's proprietary service network, which adds cost but ensures expertise. The 3-year warranty with factory installation shows confidence in reliability.

### Repair Economics
Gas range #1 repair: igniter replacement. Wolf uses silicon nitride igniters ($75-100 part) versus cheaper silicon carbide, extending lifespan 2-3x. Labor: $150-200. Total: $225-300. Factory-certified service premium justified by complexity of dual-stacked burner system requiring specialized knowledge.
