### What You Should Know
This JennAir column refrigerator represents the best built-in option on Whirlpool's platform, with genuine premium features like a variable-speed inverter compressor and Obsidian stainless interior that distinguish it from other Whirlpool brands. The reliability story has improved significantly — service rates dropped by more than half between 2021 and 2022, and importantly, these column models are not affected by the evaporator defect plaguing JennAir's French door lineup.

However, you're still getting Whirlpool-level engineering at luxury pricing. The single compressor system, while efficient, can't provide the independent cooling zones that true premium brands offer with dual compressor designs. The 12-year sealed system warranty matches Sub-Zero's coverage, but Whirlpool's recent sale of JennAir to Electrolux creates some uncertainty about future service and parts availability during the brand transition.

### Repair Economics
Variable-speed compressor failures are the most likely major repair. Compressor replacement typically costs $800-1,200 in parts plus labor, but the 12-year sealed system warranty provides extended protection. Repair is cost-justified given the warranty coverage and improved parts availability through Whirlpool's broad service network.
