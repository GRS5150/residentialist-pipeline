### Strengths
- **Best-in-class heat exchanger warranty**: 25-year residential coverage with only conditional reduction to 15 years with recirculation (vs Navien's harsh 5-year drop)

### Deficiencies
- **Thin service network**: Smaller US presence than Rinnai/Navien means fewer certified installers and potentially longer service response times
- **Limited smart features**: Basic diagnostics fall behind Navien NaviLink and Rinnai Control-R connectivity platforms
- **Parts availability concerns**: US parts network documented as thinner than Rinnai's due to smaller market share

### What You Should Know
This is a premium condensing tankless with genuinely superior warranty terms and retrofit advantages. The 25-year heat exchanger warranty is the longest available, and the top-mount connections can save hundreds in installation costs if replacing a tank unit. However, Noritz's smaller US footprint means you'll have fewer service technicians to choose from. If you have a good local Noritz installer, this unit delivers excellent value. If not, Rinnai's broader service network might be worth considering despite the shorter warranty.

### Repair Economics
Primary repair: descaling/heat exchanger cleaning ($200-300 annually). Scale buildup is the universal tankless killer. At 15+ years, major component failures (gas valve, igniter) typically cost $400-800 — often justifying replacement given technology improvements and efficiency gains.

### Score Justification
The industry-leading warranty and premium construction earn strong marks, but the service network limitation prevents Tier 1 status. This is a classic case where excellent engineering meets real-world support constraints.
