### What You Should Know
Wolf represents the premium standard for dual-stacked gas ranges, delivering both 20,000 BTU power and 500 BTU precision in the same burner. The infrared broiler provides genuine commercial searing capability. Install a range hood rated ≥600 CFM — gas combustion produces NO₂ regardless of brand. Factor in 3-year warranty with Factory Certified Installation and proprietary service requirements when budgeting total ownership cost.

### Repair Economics
Most common repairs: igniter replacement ($200-350 installed), gas valve service ($300-500), control board ($400-600). Silicon nitride igniters last 2-3x longer than commodity silicon carbide. Repairs are cost-justified on this premium product — replacement cost is $8,000-15,000+. The 50% rule supports repair through most component failures.
