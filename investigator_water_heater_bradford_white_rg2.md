### Strengths
- **Vitraglas antimicrobial lining**: Proprietary glass-enamel formula fused at 1600°F with Microban technology prevents bacterial growth on tank interior
- **ICON millivolt operation**: Functions during power outages without external electricity — genuine grid-independence advantage
- **Professional-only distribution**: Available exclusively through licensed contractors, ensuring proper sizing and installation
- **Hydrojet sediment control**: Cold-water inlet design reduces sediment buildup, extending tank life without manual flushing

### Deficiencies
- **Atmospheric gas efficiency**: 0.63 UEF is inherently limited by non-condensing technology — significantly lower than condensing alternatives
- **Standard magnesium anode**: Depletes faster in soft water conditions compared to powered titanium alternatives
- **Fixed capacity constraints**: 50-gallon tank with 81 FHR cannot match on-demand capacity of tankless systems

### What You Should Know
This is a professional-grade atmospheric gas tank built for durability over efficiency. The ICON millivolt system works during power outages — a genuine advantage in storm-prone areas. Professional-only distribution means you'll pay contractor markup but get proper installation. The Vitraglas antimicrobial lining is legitimately superior to generic glass coatings. Expect 10-15 year lifespan with proper anode maintenance.

### Repair Economics
Primary failure is tank corrosion after anode rod depletion (8-12 years). Anode replacement costs $100-150 installed. At that point, tank replacement ($1,200-1,800 installed) is usually more cost-effective than major repairs.

### Score Justification
The 80/100 reflects excellent durability engineering (millivolt operation, professional distribution) balanced against atmospheric gas efficiency limitations. Strong build quality and serviceability justify Tier 2, but efficiency ceiling prevents Tier 1 consideration.
