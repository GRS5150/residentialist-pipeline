### Company Background
Lennox International Inc. (NYSE: LII) is a publicly traded HVAC manufacturer founded in 1895, headquartered in Richardson, Texas. The company operates as a profitable, independent manufacturer without private equity backing or recent ownership changes. Lennox owns Armstrong Air (air handlers) but maintains separate product lines without badge-engineering. Manufacturing occurs primarily in North American facilities, with the company maintaining a premium market position through proprietary technology development rather than cost-focused strategies. Financial health remains strong with consistent profitability in the residential and commercial HVAC sectors.

### Strengths

### Deficiencies

### What You Should Know
The SL28XCV represents what contractors call "the Lennox Paradox" — the best specifications on paper paired with the highest total cost of ownership during service years. If you prioritize monthly energy bill savings and plan to stay in your home long-term, this unit delivers unmatched efficiency performance that can justify the higher service costs over time.

However, the iComfort communicating system locks you into Lennox's thermostat ecosystem, and while standard 24V thermostats work with adapters, you'll lose significant features. Contractors consistently recommend this unit for homeowners who prioritize energy efficiency over service cost considerations, but steer cost-conscious buyers toward alternatives with better service economics.

### Platform Disclosure

Warranty claims process rated most difficult by contractor consensus, extending repair timelines and increasing labor costs.
