Manufacturing occurs in Fort Smith, Arkansas, with the company representing a mature, established brand in the HVAC market [S5]. Rheem operates as a stable subsidiary within Paloma's portfolio, focusing on mid-market positioning rather than premium innovation. The company has maintained consistent operations without recent ownership changes or financial distress signals, positioning it as a reliable but conservative manufacturer in the residential HVAC space.

### What You Should Know
This unit represents the "Toyota Camry of HVAC" — reliable, serviceable, but not exciting. The Prestige line meaningfully outperforms Rheem's Classic series through genuine component upgrades rather than simple badge engineering. Any HVAC contractor can service this unit using readily available parts, making it an excellent choice for homeowners prioritizing long-term serviceability over cutting-edge features.

The two-stage Copeland compressor provides solid performance with better humidity control than single-stage alternatives. While the 19 SEER2 efficiency meets current standards, it won't impress efficiency enthusiasts. This unit establishes the floor for quality residential HVAC without premium aspirations.
