### Platform Disclosure
Samsung/Dacor investigation ongoing: Consumer complaints reference both Samsung and Dacor gas cooking appliances together, suggesting component sharing since Samsung's 2016 Dacor acquisition.

### Strengths
- **Parts availability confirmed**: Genuine OEM parts accessible through RepairClinic and Samsung direct with model-specific lookup and same-day shipping
- **Smart connectivity integration**: WiFi and SmartThings compatibility provides remote monitoring and control capabilities
- **Standard igniter technology**: Silicon carbide igniters are commodity components with predictable replacement cycles

### Deficiencies
- **Cross-category control board reliability pattern**: Control board failures documented consistently across Samsung dishwashers, refrigerators, wall ovens, and ranges — corporate engineering weakness
- **Service technician availability gaps**: While parts are available, finding qualified Samsung-certified technicians varies significantly by region
- **Performance limitations**: 1200 BTU simmer floor and standard sealed burners provide poor precision control compared to pro-style alternatives

### What You Should Know
This is Samsung's builder-grade gas range with smart features that add electronic complexity without addressing fundamental cooking performance. The 18,000 BTU maximum and 1200 BTU simmer represent standard sealed burner limitations. Samsung's cross-category control board reliability issues and service ecosystem gaps make this a maintenance risk. The recent electric range recall for front-mounted knob activation (250 fires, 40 injuries) raises design concerns even for gas models.

### Repair Economics
Control boards are the primary failure point for Samsung ranges, with replacement costs typically $200-400 in parts plus $150-250 labor. At $350-650 total repair cost against a mid-tier range purchase price, repair is economically justified for the first failure but becomes questionable for repeat control board issues.

### Score Justification
The 45/100 reflects Samsung's positioning between commodity appliances and legitimate cooking performance. Smart features cannot overcome fundamental durability concerns and service ecosystem limitations that consistently appear across Samsung's appliance portfolio.
