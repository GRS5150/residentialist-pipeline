### Company Background
American Olean, founded in 1923 in Lansdale, Pennsylvania, is one of the oldest ceramic tile brands in the United States and is now owned by publicly-traded Mohawk Industries (NYSE: MHK) through Dal-Tile Corporation [SRC-002]. The brand shares manufacturing infrastructure with Daltile and Marazzi USA under the same corporate umbrella but is deliberately positioned as a value tier below Daltile in both quality and design sophistication [SRC-002, SRC-003]. This positioning strategy allows Mohawk to serve different market segments while leveraging shared production capabilities and distribution networks.

### Strengths
- **Mohawk Corporate Backing**: Limited lifetime residential warranty backed by a major publicly-traded manufacturer provides reliability and support infrastructure [SRC-001]
- **Appropriate Traffic Rating**: PEI Class 3 classification with DCOF ≥0.42 on textured finishes meets requirements for light-to-moderate residential applications [SRC-001]

### Deficiencies
- **Non-Rectified Limitations**: Calibrated but not fully rectified sizing requires minimum 3/16" grout lines, limiting design flexibility and modern installation aesthetics [SRC-004]
- **Basic Quality Positioning**: Deliberately positioned below Daltile in quality and design sophistication within the same corporate family, representing entry-level rather than premium ceramic [SRC-003]

### What You Should Know
American Olean Theoretical Bold represents the "Toyota Camry" approach to ceramic tile — reliable, affordable, and backed by solid warranty support, but without premium features or design sophistication [SRC-005]. The product uses standard white body ceramic construction with vitreous water absorption characteristics, making it suitable for interior residential applications like bathrooms, backsplashes, and light-traffic floors, but not recommended for heavy-traffic areas or exterior applications [SRC-004].

Professional installers consistently describe American Olean as "perfectly respectable residential tile" that delivers consistent manufacturing quality without premium positioning [SRC-005]. The Mohawk ownership provides manufacturing reliability and warranty support, while the deliberate positioning below Daltile reflects a value-focused approach rather than cutting-edge design or superior technical specifications.

### Platform Disclosure
American Olean shares manufacturing infrastructure with Daltile and Marazzi USA under Mohawk Industries ownership, representing different quality tiers from the same production capabilities [SRC-002].

Stable — Backed by established publicly-traded Mohawk Industries with consistent manufacturing infrastructure and clear market positioning strategy.

### Repair Economics
Most common issues involve grout line maintenance due to larger spacing requirements; individual tile replacement typically costs $3-8 per square foot including labor; full replacement becomes cost-justified when repair areas exceed 30% of installation.
