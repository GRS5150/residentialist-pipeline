### Company Background
MSI (M S International) is a private company founded in 1975 and headquartered in Orange, CA. They operate as a distributor, not a manufacturer, sourcing tile, stone, quartz, and LVP from global partner factories across India, China, Turkey, Brazil, Vietnam, and other countries. With estimated revenue of $3-4 billion, MSI has grown rapidly as a value-premium distributor in the US market, selling through Floor & Decor, Home Depot, and independent tile showrooms. The company does not manufacture products but instead sources from various factories, which can change between shipments depending on pricing and availability.

### Strengths
- **Legitimate Technical Specifications**: Product meets porcelain-grade standards with <0.5% water absorption, PEI Class 4 traffic rating, and DCOF ≥0.42 slip resistance [SRC-001]

### Deficiencies
- **Manufacturing Consistency Issues**: Distributor model creates shade variation and caliper thickness inconsistencies between boxes, requiring extra sorting and leveling during installation [SRC-003, SRC-005]

### What You Should Know
MSI Aria Bianco offers legitimate porcelain specifications at a competitive price point, making it attractive for budget-conscious projects. The marble-look digital inkjet surface receives positive consumer feedback for appearance. However, as a distributed product rather than manufactured tile, consistency can vary between shipments since MSI sources from different factories depending on pricing.

For single-room installations where you're buying all material at once, this inconsistency risk is manageable. However, if you're planning a large project requiring future reorders or need guaranteed color matching across phases, the distributor model becomes problematic. Professional installers note the need for extra time sorting tiles and additional leveling compounds to address thickness variations.
