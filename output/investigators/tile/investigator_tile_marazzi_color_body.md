### Company Background
Marazzi Group was founded in 1935 in Sassuolo, Italy, and was acquired by Mohawk Industries in 2013. The Italian production facilities continue to operate from Sassuolo with distinct manufacturing standards from Marazzi USA operations, which run from separate US facilities under Mohawk ownership. This creates a critical distinction: Italian Marazzi maintains its heritage clay body formulations and firing profiles, while Marazzi USA produces different, mid-range products for the North American market. Mohawk Industries provides corporate stability as a large public flooring manufacturer, though the Italian operations maintain their premium positioning within the Mohawk portfolio.

### Strengths
- **Italian Manufacturing Heritage**: Produced in Sassuolo, the world's leading ceramic district with 90+ years of continuous innovation and 200+ manufacturers within 30km radius [SRC-003]
- **Color Body Construction**: Tinted body construction reduces chip visibility compared to standard white body tiles, with advanced digital inkjet printing technology [SRC-001]

### Deficiencies
- **Brand Confusion**: Critical distinction between Italian and US production that creates specification confusion, with US versions being mid-range quality at 2-3x lower price [SRC-004]
- **Availability Constraints**: Premium Italian lines have limited distribution compared to widely available US Marazzi products found at major retailers [SRC-005]

### What You Should Know
When buying Marazzi tile, you must verify you're getting Italian production, not the US-made version sold at Home Depot and similar retailers. The Italian-made product represents genuine Sassuolo manufacturing heritage with color body construction and superior digital printing technology that professionals specifically call out in luxury projects.

The price differential is significant—Italian Marazzi costs 2-3 times more than US Marazzi—but architects and installers consistently emphasize the quality difference is visible in tighter tolerances, richer color depth, and superior construction. This is premium Italian porcelain backed by Mohawk's corporate stability, but you're paying for authentic Sassuolo craftsmanship.
