### Company Background
Grupo Porcelanosa is a private, family-owned company founded in 1973 by Héctor and Manuel Colonques in Villarreal, Castellón, Spain [SRC-002]. With over €1 billion in annual revenue and 5,000+ employees, the company operates as a vertically integrated manufacturer controlling everything from raw materials to finished products [SRC-002]. Manufacturing occurs in Castellón province, the global capital of ceramic tile production, where Porcelanosa operates 5 million square meters of facilities as the region's flagship producer [SRC-002]. The company maintains financial stability through its family ownership structure and operates 350+ branded showrooms globally, indicating strong market positioning and distribution control [SRC-002].

This is genuine luxury tile from Spain's premier manufacturer, not a mass-market product positioned as premium. For luxury renovations where tile represents a significant investment, this approach delivers superior specification support. The 50+ year lifespan expectation reflects genuine durability — this isn't a product you'll replace during normal homeownership cycles.
