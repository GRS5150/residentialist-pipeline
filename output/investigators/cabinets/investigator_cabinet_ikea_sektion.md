## IKEA SEKTION — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 71/100 — Tier 3 Good**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 58 | Particleboard construction and thin back panels limit durability despite precision manufacturing |
| Performance | 82 | Blum OEM hardware delivers exceptional function that rivals premium brands |
| Durability | 73 | Strong institutional warranty and proven field longevity offset by moisture vulnerability |
| Material Safety | Good | Standard melamine-faced particleboard with no identified health concerns |

### Strengths
**Hardware Excellence**: SEKTION uses genuine Blum CLIP top BLUMOTION hinges tested to 200,000 cycles and full-extension steel drawer slides that professionals rate 9/10 — identical to hardware found in custom cabinetry costing thousands more.

**Dimensional Precision**: Professional installers praise the "sturdy, dimensionally accurate" boxes that achieve "solid tight fit" assembly, with one specialist firm rating overall construction 8.5/10 based on hundreds of installations.

**Long-term Value Delivery**: Field performance data shows 10+ year structural integrity in properly installed kitchens, with interior designers reporting satisfaction after 4 years of use when expectations are calibrated to price point.

### Deficiencies
**Substrate Quality Limitations**: Main Line Kitchen Design rates quality as "D" (second-lowest grade) due to particleboard construction, with professional contractors noting box construction "wasn't as sturdy as we'd prefer" compared to custom alternatives.

**Moisture Vulnerability**: The 25-year warranty specifically excludes water damage, and professionals consistently cite particleboard's inability to withstand sustained moisture exposure as the primary structural weakness.

**Thin Back Panel Construction**: Back panels measure only 3mm thick hardboard/MDF that "does not contribute structural rigidity equivalent to full-thickness panel," creating a weak point in the cabinet system.

### What You Should Know
IKEA SEKTION represents a calculated engineering trade-off: premium European hardware in budget-friendly boxes. The Blum hinges and drawer slides genuinely match what you'd find in semi-custom kitchens costing 3-4x more. However, the particleboard construction means these aren't heirloom cabinets — they're designed for 10-15 year service life. Keep them dry, especially around sinks, and they'll perform well. The metric sizing can create installation challenges in American homes.

### Score Justification
The 71/100 reflects IKEA's success at maximizing performance within severe cost constraints while acknowledging fundamental material limitations. The exceptional hardware prevents a lower score, while the particleboard substrate and moisture vulnerability prevent a higher one.
