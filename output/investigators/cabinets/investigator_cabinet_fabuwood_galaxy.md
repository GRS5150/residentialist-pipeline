## Fabuwood Galaxy — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 91/100 — Tier 1: Best in Class**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |

| Quality | 88 | Professional-grade construction with AWI Premium certification, but assembly inconsistencies reported |

### Strengths
**Premium Hardware Integration**: Galaxy uses exact-spec Blum Compact BLUMOTION hinges with 6-way adjustment and Tandem Plus undermount slides rated for 100 lbs — hardware that matches or exceeds brands costing 40% more.

**Structural Engineering Excellence**: AWI Premium Grade certification (highest available) with independent load testing up to 2,250 lbs for tall pantries, plus proprietary dovetail groove system mechanically interlocking plywood sides with solid wood face frames.

**Field Durability Performance**: Seven-year owner review shows minimal wear beyond pull-out trash cabinet, with Reddit users reporting "outstanding, no sign of wear at all" after several years of use.

### Deficiencies
**Assembly Quality Control**: BBB complaints cite color inconsistency issues, and Houzz reports document poorly executed dovetail joints in some units, indicating variable manufacturing quality despite premium certifications.

**Shipping Vulnerability**: Authorized dealer specifically warns that "damage occurs with regularity in shipping" and advises rejecting damaged cabinets, suggesting inadequate packaging protection for the quality level.

### What You Should Know
Fabuwood Galaxy delivers premium construction at mid-tier pricing — Main Line Kitchen Design rates it equal to KraftMaid quality while costing 30% less. The hardware is genuinely high-end Blum components, not knockoffs. However, you're buying assembled-in-New Jersey cabinets from flat-packed Asian components, which creates shipping risks and occasional assembly inconsistencies. The lifetime warranty upgraded from 5-year in 2023 but isn't transferable and excludes common issues like 1/4-inch door warping.

### Score Justification
The 91 score reflects exceptional value engineering — premium materials and hardware at accessible pricing, backed by legitimate third-party certifications. It doesn't score higher due to documented assembly variability and shipping damage issues that premium domestic brands avoid.
