## Merillat Classic — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 64/100 — Tier 3: Good**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 58 | Construction shortcuts and QC issues drag down an otherwise adequate build |
| Performance | 61 | Commodity hardware limits function despite acceptable structural basics |
| Durability | 73 | Solid face frame and 25-year warranty offset weaker joinery and hardware concerns |
| Material Safety | Good | Standard engineered wood construction with no flagged concerns |

### Strengths
- **Solid hardwood face frame construction**: 3/4-inch solid hardwood provides structural integrity and professional appearance, distinguishing it from frameless competitors
- **Fast delivery advantage**: FastTrack 2-week lead time offers genuine competitive advantage for builders and time-sensitive projects
- **Upgrade path available**: Deluxe tier options (plywood construction, dovetail drawers, undermount slides) can partially close performance gaps

### Deficiencies
- **Thin cabinet box construction**: 3/8-inch particleboard sides are notably thinner than 1/2-inch industry standard, creating structural weakness under heavy loads
- **Commodity drawer hardware**: Standard 3/4-extension roller slides with plastic components fail over time, and Merillat discontinued individual replacement parts
- **Quality control inconsistencies**: Professional installers report door warping, finish inconsistencies, and assembly gaps that required warranty claims

### What You Should Know
Merillat Classic sits at the bottom of the semi-custom market with builder-grade construction that's adequate for normal use but shows compromises under scrutiny. The standard spec uses thinner cabinet sides and weaker drawer joinery than competitors, though the solid hardwood face frame is genuinely well-built. Most professionals recommend the plywood upgrade to achieve acceptable quality levels. The brand lost KCMA certification in 2022 and hasn't re-certified, while parent company financial stress adds long-term support concerns.

### Score Justification
The 64-point score reflects a product that meets basic functional requirements but falls short of semi-custom expectations without upgrades. Structural shortcuts prevent a higher score, while the solid face frame and warranty coverage keep it from dropping to builder-grade territory.
