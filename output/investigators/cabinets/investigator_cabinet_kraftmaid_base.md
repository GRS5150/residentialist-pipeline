## KraftMaid Base Configuration — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 80/100 — Tier 2: Excellent**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 75 | Strong hardware and drawer construction undermined by particleboard box substrate |
| Performance | 88 | Exceptional Blum hardware specs (200K hinge cycles, 100K slide cycles) with robust structural testing |
| Durability | 77 | Solid warranty coverage offset by documented claim denials and moisture vulnerability |
| Material Safety | Good | Standard wood and engineered materials with catalyzed finish system |

### Strengths
**Premium Hardware Integration**: KraftMaid ships with industry-leading Blum Whisper Touch hinges tested to 200,000 cycles and undermount slides rated for 100,000 cycles at 90-lb capacity — hardware specs that exceed most competitors in this price class.

**Exceptional Drawer Construction**: 3/4-inch solid hardwood dovetail construction on all four corners with captured 3/16-inch plywood bottoms represents best-in-class drawer box quality that professionals consistently praise.

**Robust Structural Engineering**: I-beam base supports tested to 2,500 lbs and wall cabinets rated to 600 lbs static load demonstrate serious structural engineering that outperforms typical semi-custom specifications.

### Deficiencies
**Particleboard Box Liability**: Standard configuration ships with 1/2-inch engineered wood furniture board (not plywood) for all box panels, which professionals identify as the primary quality limitation requiring expensive plywood upgrade to match all-plywood competitors like Fabuwood.

**Warranty Execution Problems**: Despite lifetime coverage, documented cases show KraftMaid denying claims by attributing damage to moisture or installation issues, with no labor cost coverage making even successful claims financially burdensome for homeowners.

**Quality Control Issues**: Shipping damage is a consistent complaint pattern, with documented cases like 13 damaged doors in a single 26-door delivery indicating manufacturing or logistics quality control problems.

### What You Should Know
KraftMaid delivers premium hardware and drawer construction on a standard particleboard box that professionals consistently identify as the weak link. The company uses genuinely excellent Blum hardware and solid wood dovetail drawers, but the particleboard substrate makes the cabinets vulnerable to moisture damage around sinks and dishwashers over 5-10 years. You'll need to upgrade to all-plywood construction to get quality that matches competitors like Fabuwood who ship plywood standard at lower prices. The warranty is lifetime but has broad exclusions and no labor coverage, with documented claim denial patterns.

### Score Justification
The 80-point score reflects genuinely premium components (hardware, drawers, structural engineering) compromised by cost-cutting on the box substrate. This prevents a higher score despite excellent hardware, while the strong engineering and professional construction keeps it from scoring lower despite the particleboard limitation.
