## Waterstone — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 91/100 — Tier 1 — Best in Class**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 94 | Exceptional body materials and true manufacturing credentials, but lacks modern PVD finish technology |
| Durability | 83 | Strong functional warranty and metal construction offset by documented finish durability issues |
| Performance | 92 | Metal spray wands and quality cartridges deliver reliable operation |
| Material Safety | Excellent | NSF 61/372 certified, lead-free Eco-Brass, no safety concerns identified |

### Strengths
**Best-in-category body construction**: 316 marine-grade stainless steel machined from solid bar stock in monoblock construction eliminates casting defects and voids entirely — this is the premium construction method in the faucet category.

**True domestic manufacturer**: Designs, engineers, machines, finishes, and assembles at their Murrieta, California facility with FTC Made in USA qualification — not an assembler or specifier like many "luxury" brands.

**Metal spray wands**: All spray components are metal construction rather than plastic, which exceeds category standards where most competitors including Brizo use plastic components.

### Deficiencies
**Complete absence of PVD finish technology**: Every colored finish uses powder coat or lacquer described as "semi-durable" by Waterstone itself, creating finishes 10-20x less scratch-resistant than PVD competitors like California Faucets.

**Documented finish failures in field**: Finish deterioration is the most common failure mode with documented cases of tarnishing on day one and deterioration within two years, despite 7-year warranties on most finishes.

**Cartridge limitation**: Uses Geann Industrial cartridges across all configurations — solid mid-tier supplier but not the premium Flühs cartridges found in top competitors.

### What You Should Know
Waterstone represents the rare true manufacturer in a category dominated by assemblers and marketers. Their body construction using 316 marine-grade stainless steel machined from bar stock is legitimately best-in-class. However, they've made a strategic decision to avoid PVD finish technology entirely, leaving all colored finishes vulnerable to premature wear. If you're buying chrome or stainless steel (which get lifetime finish warranties), you're getting exceptional value. For colored finishes, expect durability issues that the company openly acknowledges. The transferable lifetime functional warranty is unusual and valuable for resale.

### Score Justification
The 91 score reflects exceptional manufacturing credentials and body construction that few competitors match, balanced against the finish technology gap that creates real durability concerns. Higher scores require both premium construction AND modern finish technology — Waterstone delivers only half that equation.
