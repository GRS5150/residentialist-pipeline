### Company Background
Brizo is owned by Delta Faucet Company, a subsidiary of Masco Corporation (NYSE: MAS), a publicly-traded building products manufacturer founded in 1929. Delta manufactures over two-thirds of Brizo faucets in China, with less than one-third made in the US, representing a significant manufacturing migration that has moved the brand from "North American Luxury" to "Asia-assembled luxury" classification [SRC-005]. Brizo shares core DST cartridge technology with sibling brands Delta and Peerless, but differentiates through brass construction and premium PVD finishes versus Delta's ZAMAK bodies and Peerless's budget materials. The brand benefits from Masco's financial stability and established supply chain, but the manufacturing shift reflects cost-optimization priorities typical of large public companies.

### Strengths
- **Diamond Seal Technology Cartridge**: Proprietary cartridge with diamond-dust embedded discs tested to 5 million cycles (10x ASME standard), equivalent to 700 years of daily use, with self-polishing action that eliminates mineral deposits and lubricant needs [SRC-002]
- **Brilliance PVD Finishes**: Vapor Technologies-developed finishes withstand 100+ household cleaners and steel wool scouring, guaranteed for life against corrosion, tarnishing, and discoloration [SRC-003]

### Deficiencies
- **Inconsistent Cartridge Quality**: Not all Brizo models use the premium DST cartridge—some use lower-grade Sedal cartridges or unmarked cartridges of unknown origin and quality [SRC-004]
- **Plastic Construction Elements**: All kitchen spray wands use plastic construction, falling below competitors like Waterstone that provide metal wands in the luxury segment [SRC-007]

### What You Should Know
Brizo represents a premium brand that has undergone significant manufacturing changes, with over two-thirds of production now in China rather than North America. The brand's reputation rests heavily on its Diamond Seal Technology cartridge, which offers industry-leading durability when present. However, buyers must specifically verify DST cartridge presence before purchase, as not all Brizo models include this premium component.

StarCraft Independent Reviews recommends Brizo as "Best Value in luxury faucets made or assembled in Asia" but emphasizes the importance of model verification [SRC-001]. Professional plumbers rate DST-equipped models highly for ease of repair and cartridge replacement, though some design-specific installation challenges exist on certain models like the Litze line [SRC-010]. The lifetime warranty and best-in-class customer service provide additional value protection for buyers willing to navigate the model selection complexity.

### Repair Economics
Most common failure involves cartridge replacement after extended use. DST cartridges are provided free under lifetime warranty to original owners, making repair cost-effective. Easy cartridge replacement design reduces professional service costs, though proprietary nature limits third-party repair options [SRC-006, SRC-010].
