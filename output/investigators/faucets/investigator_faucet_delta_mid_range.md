## Delta Mid-Range (DST Models) — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 69/100 — Tier 3: Good**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 66 | Innovative PEX waterway design undermined by ZAMAK zinc shell construction |
| Durability | 78 | Outstanding cartridge technology and industry-leading parts availability |
| Performance | 70 | Standard regulated flow delivery with reliable operation |

### Strengths
**Industry-Leading Cartridge Technology**: Uses the same proprietary DST (Diamond Seal Technology) cartridge as premium Brizo faucets — Kerox and Maruwa ceramic discs with diamond coating, independently tested to 5 million cycles, far exceeding standard cartridge longevity.

**Unmatched Parts Infrastructure**: Strongest parts availability in the industry through massive distribution network (Home Depot, Lowe's, Ferguson, all major plumbing supply houses), backed by Masco's scale and lifetime warranty with free cartridge replacements.

**Professional Plumber Endorsement**: Consistently ranked #1 or #2 recommended brand by professional plumbers across independent polls, praised specifically for DST reliability and ease of service.

### Deficiencies
**Zinc Shell Construction**: Decorative shell is die-cast ZAMAK zinc alloy rather than solid brass — functional but not equivalent to luxury-grade construction, placing it "firmly below luxury threshold" according to expert analysis.

**Manufacturing Migration**: Transformed from primarily US manufacturer to designer/brand assembler outsourcing majority fabrication to Asian OEMs — quality maintained through specifications but no longer owned manufacturing.

**Product Line Confusion**: Not all Delta models use DST technology — Classics and Foundations collections use inferior legacy ball valve systems, requiring buyers to specifically verify "-DST suffix models only."

### What You Should Know
Delta's DST models represent genuine engineering innovation at mass-market pricing, delivering the same advanced cartridge technology found in premium brands. The PEX waterway design is clever — water never contacts the outer zinc shell, flowing through continuous tubing from supply to spout. While the zinc construction isn't luxury-grade, the functional engineering is solid and the parts support is unmatched. Professional plumbers consistently recommend Delta because it works reliably and is easy to service when needed.

### Score Justification
The 69 score reflects good engineering housed in non-premium materials — innovative PEX waterway and proven 5-million-cycle cartridge technology offset by ZAMAK shell construction that keeps it out of luxury territory despite functional excellence.
