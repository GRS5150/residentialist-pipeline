## In2aqua — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 92/100 — Tier 1: Best in Class**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 88 | Outstanding cartridge engineering but assembler business model limits score ceiling |
| Durability | 98 | Industry-leading cartridge technology and warranty create exceptional longevity |
| Performance | 85 | Meets all regulatory standards with WaterSense certification |
| Material Safety | Excellent | Solid brass construction with compliant low-lead alloy |

### Strengths
**Kerox PVD+ Cartridge Innovation**: The diamond-like carbon coating on ceramic discs tested to 4 million cycles (560 years equivalent) with no visible wear eliminates lubricant dependency — the primary failure mode in conventional cartridges. This is a genuine engineering breakthrough.

**Industry's Best Warranty**: GoPro lifetime warranty covers the entire faucet including cartridge with no cartridge exclusions, backed by same-day parts dispatch from California logistics center. StarCraft confirms this as the strongest warranty in the industry.

**All-Brass M-Lock Collar**: Proprietary brass cartridge retaining collar where most competitors use plastic that can flex under pressure, causing cartridge deformation and leaks.

### Deficiencies
**Assembler/Specifier Business Model**: Not a traditional manufacturer — sources components from Kerox (cartridges), Flühs Drehtechnik (stems/casting), and others. This limits control over the entire production process.

**No PVD Finish Technology**: Lacks the advanced Physical Vapor Deposition finishes offered by premium competitors, limiting finish durability and selection to four basic options versus broader ranges from California Faucets or Waterstone.

**Limited Long-Term Field Data**: North American market entry in 2014 means less than 12 years of real-world performance data compared to established competitors.

### What You Should Know
In2aqua delivers genuine innovation through their PVD+ cartridge technology that could theoretically last centuries, not decades. The lifetime warranty with no cartridge exclusions is unmatched in the industry. However, you're buying from an assembler, not a manufacturer — they source premium components from German and Swiss suppliers rather than controlling production end-to-end. The finish options are limited and lack advanced PVD technology. StarCraft has rated them "Best Value in European Luxury" for seven consecutive years, suggesting consistent quality despite being newer to North America.

### Score Justification
The 92 score reflects revolutionary cartridge engineering and warranty coverage that sets new industry standards, offset by assembler business model limitations and finish technology gaps. The PVD+ innovation alone justifies Tier 1 status, but manufacturing control constraints prevent a perfect score.
