### Strengths
- **Category-leading cartridge technology**: DST cartridge with Kerox/Maruwa ceramic discs and diamond-dust self-polishing technology independently tested to 5 million cycles (10x ASME standard), manufactured at Delta's Kentucky facility
- **Superior finish durability**: Brilliance PVD finishes by Vapor Technologies withstand drain cleaners, 100+ household products, and steel wool scouring with lifetime guarantee against corrosion
- **Advanced spray technology**: MagneDock rare-earth magnet system and ShieldSpray targeting with professional plumber praise for easy cartridge serviceability

### Deficiencies
- **Manufacturing migration impact**: Now over two-thirds China production caused StarCraft to reclassify from "Best Value NA Luxury" to Asian category, losing domestic manufacturing advantage
- **Product line inconsistency**: Not all Brizo models use DST cartridge — some use Sedal cartridges from China or completely unknown cartridges with no quality assurance
- **Plastic spray wands**: All kitchen models use plastic construction while premium competitor Waterstone uses metal wands

### What You Should Know
Brizo represents Masco's luxury brand built around their exceptional DST cartridge technology — the best-tested cartridge in the industry. However, manufacturing has shifted predominantly to China, losing the brand's original North American manufacturing advantage. The critical buying decision is verifying DST cartridge presence, as some Brizo models use inferior or unknown cartridges. The Brilliance PVD finishes are genuinely lifetime-durable, and Masco's warranty support is excellent. Professional plumbers rate DST models highly for reliability and serviceability.

### Score Justification
The 84 score reflects world-class cartridge engineering and finish technology compromised by manufacturing migration and product line inconsistency. The DST cartridge alone justifies premium positioning, but the shift to majority-China production and presence of non-DST models within the same brand prevents higher scoring.
