### Company Background
California Faucets operates a 180-person manufacturing facility in Huntington Beach, California, producing hand-assembled luxury faucets since the artisan era. The company was recently acquired by American Bath Group, a private equity-backed portfolio company, representing a shift from family-owned independent to PE ownership [SRC-007]. Manufacturing remains US-based with craft shop methodology rather than mass production assembly lines [SRC-011]. The company has established itself as a luxury brand with pricing 20-30% below comparable European luxury competitors while maintaining premium positioning [SRC-008].

### Strengths
- **European Cartridge Supremacy**: Exclusive dual-sourcing from Flühs Drehtechnik (Germany) for stems and Kerox Kft (Hungary) for mixers — considered the industry's best cartridge combination with 500,000+ cycle ratings [SRC-002, SRC-004, SRC-005]
- **Lifetime PVD Warranty**: Molecular-level PVD bonding produces hardness 5-10x greater than chrome with lifetime guarantee coverage [SRC-009]

### What You Should Know
California Faucets represents American artisan manufacturing at its finest, with each unit hand-assembled and finished individually rather than mass-produced. The company's partnership with Europe's premier cartridge manufacturers — Flühs from Germany and Kerox from Hungary — gives you access to the same components found in the world's most expensive luxury faucets. StarCraft Independent Reviews awarded near-perfect scores and named it Best Value Faucet in North American Luxury for both 2023 and 2025.

The recent private equity acquisition by American Bath Group signals corporate transition, though manufacturing remains in California with the same craft-focused approach. With pricing 20-30% below European luxury competitors and extensive customization options, this represents exceptional value in the luxury faucet category. The lifetime PVD warranty and stellar customer service ratings provide additional confidence, though buyers should be aware of isolated social media corrosion complaints that haven't been professionally verified.
