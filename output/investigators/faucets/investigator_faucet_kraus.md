## Kraus — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 45/100 — Tier 4: Fair**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 42 | Marketeer business model with unknown factory sourcing creates fundamental quality inconsistency |
| Durability | 38 | Five-year cartridge warranty and parts guarantee versus lifetime warranties from competitors |
| Performance | 65 | Legal certified faucet that delivers water adequately with user-replaceable cartridges |
| Material Safety | Good | Lead-free brass construction meets federal and state safety requirements |

### Strengths
- **Cartridge lottery upside**: Some models contain world-class Kerox (Hungary) or Flühs (Germany) cartridges that rival premium brands when you get lucky with the sourcing
- **Legal certification gate**: Full UPC/cUPC, NSF 61/372, and ASME compliance means this isn't contraband Amazon flooding — it's a legitimate installable product
- **Solid brass construction**: Above-average build quality for Chinese-origin product with confirmed lead-free brass body construction

### Deficiencies  
- **Cartridge transparency crisis**: Kraus sources from 4+ different cartridge suppliers but won't disclose which cartridge is in which faucet model, creating a quality lottery for consumers
- **Warranty structure gutted**: Five-year cartridge warranty versus lifetime from Moen/Delta/Grohe, plus handles and base plates carry NO warranty coverage at all
- **Parts availability cliff**: Only 5-year parts guarantee with no manufacturing control means potential serviceability dead-end after warranty expires

### What You Should Know
Kraus operates as a pure importer and marketeer with no manufacturing facilities, sourcing from 3-4 unidentified Chinese factories. You're buying a quality lottery ticket — some faucets contain excellent European cartridges while others get lower-tier components, and Kraus won't tell you which. The "limited lifetime" warranty is misleading because key components like handles have zero coverage and cartridges only get 5 years versus lifetime warranties from established brands. This sits above commodity Amazon contraband but below any brand with actual manufacturing accountability.

### Score Justification
The 45 score reflects a legitimate but compromised product — it's legally certified and functional, but the marketeer business model, cartridge transparency failure, and gutted warranty structure prevent any higher scoring. The solid brass construction and occasional premium cartridges keep it from dropping into contraband territory.
