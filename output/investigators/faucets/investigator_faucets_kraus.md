### Company Background
Kraus USA is a pure importer and marketeer founded as an import business, not a manufacturer [SRC-005]. The company operates from Port Washington, NY headquarters with no manufacturing facility [SRC-010]. Kraus sources faucets from 3-4 Chinese factories but does not disclose specific factory names or locations, maintaining no manufacturing control over the supply chain [SRC-005, SRC-010]. The company does not design, engineer, or manufacture products — it simply imports and rebrands Chinese-made faucets for the US market [SRC-001, SRC-005]. This marketeer model means buyers are purchasing a rebadged product with limited corporate oversight of quality control and component sourcing.

### Strengths

### Deficiencies
- **Unknown Cartridge Quality**: Multiple unidentified cartridge suppliers across catalog create reliability uncertainty, with StarCraft unable to guarantee cartridge quality [SRC-001, SRC-002]

### What You Should Know
Kraus operates as a pure import and marketing company, not a manufacturer, sourcing from multiple Chinese factories without disclosing which components come from which suppliers. While the brass construction appears solid and meets safety certifications, the critical cartridge components vary wildly in quality across models — ranging from world-class Hungarian Kerox to generic Chinese suppliers — and Kraus won't tell you which cartridge is in your specific faucet.

The warranty structure reveals the company's limitations: only 5 years on cartridges versus lifetime warranties from established manufacturers, and no warranty at all on handles or base plates. Parts availability is guaranteed for just 5 years, after which you're on your own. Professional plumbers view Kraus as a step above commodity Amazon products but below established brands, with mixed opinions on long-term reliability.

### Platform Disclosure
Kraus sources from 3-4 Chinese factories with shared manufacturing across multiple import brands, though specific factory partnerships are not disclosed [SRC-005, SRC-010].

### Corporate Outlook

### Repair Economics
Most common failure is cartridge replacement ($29.95 for Kerox K-35 equivalent), but unknown cartridge type requires identification before ordering parts, and availability not guaranteed beyond 5 years [SRC-007].
