### Company Background
Cosentino is a Spanish global manufacturer founded as a family business and now operating as a major international corporation with manufacturing facilities primarily in Spain. The company has established itself as a premium surface manufacturer with a strong financial position, offering lifetime residential warranties on Dekton products. Cosentino operates dedicated manufacturing for their sintered stone products using proprietary high-temperature/high-pressure processes, distinguishing it from badge-engineered competitors.

### Strengths
- **Exceptional Heat Resistance**: Continuously rated for 500°F+ with temporary exposure tolerance to higher temperatures, significantly outperforming quartz and solid surface materials [SRC-001]
- **Superior Stain Resistance**: Non-porous factory finish resists wine, coffee, lemon, turmeric, and other problem substances without requiring sealing [SRC-003]
- **UV Stability for Outdoor Use**: Confirmed rating for outdoor applications with documented 5+ year field performance, unlike quartz competitors [SRC-005]

### Deficiencies
- **Edge Fragility During Fabrication**: Chipping at edges reported in fabrication community, creating higher material waste and requiring skilled installation [SRC-004]
- **Field Cracking Reports**: Cracking observed near cooktops in some installations, likely from direct flame contact on thin 2cm material [SRC-001]

### What You Should Know
Dekton represents the premium tier of sintered stone technology, manufactured by pressing glass, porcelain, and quartz at extreme temperatures and pressures to create an ultra-compact surface. The manufacturing process eliminates porosity and creates exceptional resistance to heat, stains, and UV exposure that makes it suitable for both indoor and outdoor applications where other engineered surfaces would fail.

However, the material's hardness creates fabrication challenges that require diamond tooling and skilled installers, potentially increasing project costs. While the surface itself is nearly indestructible under normal use, field reports of cracking near high-heat sources and edge chipping during installation suggest careful attention to fabricator selection and installation practices.

### Platform Disclosure
Sintered stone manufacturing by Cosentino uses proprietary processes distinct from competitors like Lapitec and Neolith.

### Repair Economics
Most common issues involve edge chipping during fabrication rather than in-use failures. When damage occurs, repairs typically require professional restoration or section replacement due to the material's hardness. The lifetime warranty coverage helps offset potential repair costs for manufacturing defects.
