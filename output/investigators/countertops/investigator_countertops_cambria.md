### Company Background
Cambria is family-owned by the Davis family and operates from a single manufacturing facility in Le Sueur, Minnesota. This single-plant production model contrasts with competitors like Caesarstone who operate multi-plant operations across Georgia, Israel, India, and China [SRC-003]. The family ownership structure and concentrated manufacturing approach allows for tighter quality control and consistency compared to brands with distributed production networks. As a premium US-manufactured brand, Cambria has maintained its positioning in the high-end engineered quartz market while avoiding the ownership changes and manufacturing shifts common among competitors.

### What You Should Know
Cambria represents the premium tier of engineered quartz with 93% quartz content and single-source US manufacturing that delivers exceptional consistency. The Davis family's ownership and Le Sueur facility concentration means your countertops come from the same production line with identical specifications, eliminating the lot-to-lot variance that can occur with multi-facility operations.

However, be aware that Cambria faced chipping issues serious enough to trigger class-action litigation before 2020. While the company has since reformulated their product, this represents a significant durability failure that required legal resolution. The current formula has earned fabricator consensus as the industry's most consistent engineered quartz, but the historical issues demonstrate that even premium brands can experience manufacturing defects that affect thousands of installations.

Engineered quartz chips represent the most common failure mode, typically requiring professional repair ranging from $200-500 for minor chips to full slab replacement ($1,500-3,000) for major damage. Given Cambria's premium positioning and single-source manufacturing, replacement costs justify repair attempts for most damage scenarios.
