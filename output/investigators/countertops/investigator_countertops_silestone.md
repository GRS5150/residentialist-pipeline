### Company Background
Silestone is manufactured by Cosentino, a Spanish family-owned company founded in 1979 that has grown into a global surfaces leader. The company operates multiple production facilities including their primary plant in Cantoria, Spain (highest quality control), a secondary facility in Sdot Yam, Israel (shared with Caesarstone), and a recently expanded plant in Marietta, GA, along with operations in India and China [SRC-002]. Cosentino maintains strong brand recognition and good distribution networks, though they rank slightly below Caesarstone in fabricator preference surveys. The company has invested in sustainability through their HybriQ+ technology using recycled materials and claims N-Boost technology, though independent verification remains limited.

### Strengths
- **Multi-Facility Quality Control**: Primary production from Cantoria, Spain facility delivers EU-sourced materials with highest quality control standards [SRC-002]
- **Professional Repairability**: Edge chips can be invisibly repaired with matching epoxy, and professional cleaning effectively addresses most staining issues [SRC-007]

### Deficiencies
- **UV Vulnerability**: Indoor use only due to documented fading in direct sunlight, particularly affecting dark colors like Espresso and Black Storm [SRC-003]
- **Chemical Sensitivity**: Susceptible to damage from undiluted bleach (discoloration, resin degradation) and acetone/solvents causing visible dulling [SRC-004]

### What You Should Know
Silestone offers solid performance as an engineered quartz surface with competitive hardness (7-7.5 Mohs) and heat resistance (350-400°F), matching industry leaders like Caesarstone in basic specifications. However, this is strictly an indoor-only product — direct sunlight exposure will cause fading, especially in darker color selections. The material requires careful maintenance, as common household items like nail polish remover or undiluted bleach can cause permanent damage.

With proper care, you can expect 20-25 years of service life, though edge chipping may require professional repair by years 15-18. The most likely issue you'll encounter is edge chipping, which is repairable, though seam separation can occur with multi-slab installations depending on fabricator skill. Color consistency may vary depending on which facility produced your slab, with the Spanish facility offering the most consistent quality.
