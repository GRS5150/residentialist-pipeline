### Platform Disclosure
This dishwasher shares the same platform with KitchenAid, Maytag, Amana, JennAir, Kenmore, and IKEA models. The platform is manufactured in Findlay, Ohio with WCM certification. KitchenAid variants add SatinGlide Max rails, 360° Max Jets, ProDry fan technology, and premium finishes to this base platform.

### Strengths
- **Motor Quality**: PMSM brushless motor from Askoll (part W11612327) provides efficient, reliable operation at 47 dBA noise level — significantly quieter than the 53 dBA initially specified.
- **Service Ecosystem**: Outstanding parts availability with commodity drain pump (W11035709, $35-47) shared across the massive Whirlpool platform, enabling 30-minute DIY repairs.
- **Water Efficiency**: 2.5 gallons per cycle leads the calibration set for water conservation, combined with very good washing performance per Consumer Reports.

### Deficiencies  
- **Drying Performance**: Consumer Reports rates drying at 1/5 (worst possible) due to basic recessed heating element technology, requiring manual door cracking workaround.
- **UI Reliability**: Touchpad moisture ingress issue where membrane lifts and admits moisture, causing shorts requiring $85-150 repair.
- **No Energy Star**: 270 kWh/year consumption disqualifies it from Energy Star certification, driven by inefficient heated drying system.

### What You Should Know
This dishwasher represents the sweet spot of the Whirlpool platform — solid washing performance with excellent repairability at a reasonable price. The major compromise is drying performance, which is genuinely poor and will require you to manually crack the door or towel-dry plastics. The PMSM motor and stainless tub construction exceed expectations for this price range, and when it does break, parts are cheap and widely available.

### Repair Economics
Most common failure is drain pump after several years of use. Parts cost $35-47 (W11035709), total repair cost $120-200 with service call. Given the low parts cost and universal availability, repairs are cost-justified throughout the product's lifespan. UI touchpad replacement runs $85-150 in parts plus labor.

### Score Justification
The 64 score reflects strong durability fundamentals (1.4-4.1% historical service rates, excellent parts ecosystem) offset by a genuine performance weakness in drying. This isn't a premium appliance, but it's a competent workhorse with manageable compromises.
