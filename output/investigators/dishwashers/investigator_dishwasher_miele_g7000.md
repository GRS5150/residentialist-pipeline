### Strengths
- **Motor Manufacturing Excellence**: In-house brushless DC motor (PN 6060101) manufactured at Miele Euskirchen using custom ArcelorMittal electrical steel from Eisenhüttenstadt and St-Chély d'Apcher facilities, representing the highest level of vertical integration in the dishwasher category.
- **Service Ecosystem Dominance**: 5.6% service rate makes this the most reliable brand in Yale's 33,190-call database, with industry-unique 15-year parts guarantee ensuring long-term serviceability when others discontinue support.
- **Whisper-Quiet Operation**: 39 dBA (G7766) and 40 dBA (G7566) operation levels with Extra Quiet mode reaching 37 dBA, among the quietest residential dishwashers available.

### Deficiencies
- **AutoDos Reliability Legacy**: AutoDos 1.0 system generated F44/F45/F600 error codes from detergent hardening and moisture intrusion, contributing to Miele's 2020 service rate spike to 20.5% before engineering corrections.
- **Drying Performance Gap**: Yale Appliance rates Miele drying as "very good" while rating Bosch CrystalDry as "superior" for plastic items, representing the one performance area where this premium product trails competition.
- **Repair Cost Reality**: Circulation pump failures cost approximately $1,000 total ($800 parts + $200 labor), making major repairs expensive even with excellent parts availability.

### What You Should Know
This is the most reliable dishwasher you can buy, with a 5.6% service rate that dramatically improved from early AutoDos issues. The in-house motor manufacturing represents genuine engineering superiority, not marketing. At 39-40 dBA, it's quieter than most refrigerators. The 15-year parts guarantee is industry-unique and reflects Miele's confidence in long-term support. While expensive to repair when major components fail, the low failure rate and exceptional service ecosystem justify the premium.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is drain pump failure (F11 error) at $85 for parts plus labor, typically under $200 total. Major circulation pump repairs reach $1,000 but are rare given the 5.6% service rate. Repair costs are justified on this product due to the 20-year design life and 15-year parts availability guarantee.

### Score Justification
The 95/100 score reflects best-in-class reliability metrics with genuine manufacturing advantages, offset only by AutoDos legacy issues and trailing drying performance. This represents the ceiling for current dishwasher technology.
