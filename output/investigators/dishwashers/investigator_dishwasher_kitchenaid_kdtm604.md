### Platform Disclosure
This KitchenAid model shares the core Findlay, Ohio platform with Whirlpool, Maytag, Amana, JennAir, Kenmore, and IKEA dishwashers. KitchenAid adds exclusive features including SatinGlide Max dual ball-bearing rack glides, the 360° Max Jets third rack system, and ProDry fan-assisted drying, but the fundamental motor, pump, and control systems are shared across the platform.

### Strengths
- **Self-cleaning filter system**: True Self-Cleaning Filtration flushes debris during drain cycles, eliminating manual mesh cleaning that plagues many dishwashers
- **Comprehensive service ecosystem**: Commodity drain pump (W11035709) available for $32-71, circulation pump readily available at $80 OEM, and universal technician familiarity with Whirlpool platform
- **Premium hydraulics**: 4-level spray system with unique 360° rotating jets in the largest third rack configuration creates superior wash coverage

### Deficiencies
- **Sump seal catastrophic failure**: Diverter motor shaft seal fails frequently but seal is not sold separately, forcing full sump+motor assembly replacement at $500-700 total cost
- **Traditional motor technology**: PSC AC induction motor with run capacitor (W10753070) lacks the efficiency and longevity of brushless inverter systems found in premium tiers
- **Plastic drying performance**: ProDry heated-element system works well for plates but struggles with plastics, leaving residual moisture despite fan assistance

### What You Should Know
This is a well-engineered dishwasher built on America's most widely serviced platform, with excellent cleaning performance and genuinely useful premium features. The 8.2% service rate is manageable, and most repairs are affordable with universal parts availability. However, the sump seal failure is a design flaw that creates an expensive forced replacement scenario. The traditional motor technology and heated drying limitations keep it from premium performance levels.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is sump/pump assembly replacement due to diverter motor shaft seal leak ($500-700 total). The seal cannot be purchased separately, making this a full assembly replacement. At this price point, repair is cost-justified, but the failure mode represents poor serviceability design.

### Score Justification
The 81 score reflects strong fundamentals undermined by specific weaknesses. Excellent platform serviceability and genuine feature innovation earn the Tier 2 rating, but the sump seal design flaw and traditional drying technology prevent higher scoring.
