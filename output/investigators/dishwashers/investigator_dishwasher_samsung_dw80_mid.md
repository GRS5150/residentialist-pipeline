### Strengths
- **Motor Technology**: Samsung DD82 series BLDC inverter motor (in-house design) provides quiet operation at 46 dBA and proven circulation reliability across the mid-range lineup.
- **Construction Foundation**: Stainless steel tub (STSS designation) with lifetime leakage warranty and digital leak sensor system provides structural integrity that outlasts the electronics.

### Deficiencies
- **Service Ecosystem Collapse**: Yale Appliance dropped Samsung entirely due to "nonexistent customer service and repair network" — parts are available but finding qualified technicians is nearly impossible in many markets.
- **Rack System Degradation**: Class action investigation underway for rack coating defects appearing at 12-24 months, progressing to rust by 2-3 years with warranty claims routinely denied as "cosmetic."
- **Control Board Complexity**: Dual-board diagnostic system complicates repairs while Samsung's contracted technician network shows systematic coverage gaps and below-market compensation driving tech avoidance.

### What You Should Know
This dishwasher represents Samsung's fundamental appliance problem: decent engineering undermined by catastrophic post-purchase support. The 23% Consumer Reports failure rate wouldn't be fatal if repairs were accessible, but Samsung's service network has effectively collapsed in many markets. While parts remain available through multiple channels, finding technicians willing to service Samsung appliances has become the primary constraint. The stainless steel tub construction is solid, but rack coating failures within two years are widespread and warranty execution is consistently adversarial.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is control board failure ($180-250 parts + $200-300 labor when you can find service). Total repair cost of $450+ approaches 75% of unit replacement cost. Repair is rarely cost-justified given the likelihood of additional failures and ongoing service access problems.

### Score Justification
The 47/100 score reflects a product where baseline functionality works but ownership becomes untenable when problems arise. Samsung's service ecosystem collapse transforms every repair into a multi-week ordeal, making this dishwasher unsuitable despite acceptable performance when functioning.
