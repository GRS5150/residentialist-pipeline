### Platform Disclosure
The Bosch 800 Series shares the same platform architecture with Thermador and Gaggenau dishwashers. All three brands use identical control boards, circulation pumps, drain pumps, filters, sumps, and spray arms, with service rates within 0.4% of each other (7.7-8.1%). Price differentiation comes from features and finishes, not structural components.

### Strengths
- **CrystalDry zeolite technology** delivers 40% better plastic drying than AutoAir systems, achieving 100% drying performance including plastics at 176°F chamber temperature
- **Exceptional serviceability** with plug-and-play parts, documented test mode, and reasonable part costs ($131.50 circulation pump, $43.74 drain pump via PartSelect)
- **Industry-leading reliability** with 7.8% service rate placing it second only to Miele, showing consistent improvement trend

### Deficiencies
- **Control board heater relay failure** is a multi-generation issue since 2012, typically occurring in years 3-7 with $160 UpFix repair cost
- **Motor screen blockage** emerging on 2023-2024 production units, with technicians noting quality concerns on recent models
- **Energy consumption penalty** from zeolite regeneration pushes annual usage to 240 kWh versus 199 kWh on non-zeolite models

### What You Should Know
This is the sweet spot in Bosch's lineup — you get the premium CrystalDry drying technology and ball-bearing rack glides without paying the Benchmark premium. The 42 dBA noise level makes it nearly silent, and the flexible third rack with fold-down wings maximizes loading versatility. The main concern is the known control board relay issue that typically surfaces around year 4, but the part is readily available and repairable.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is E24 drainage failure from filter neglect ($30 valve replacement). The signature failure is control board relay fatigue ($160 UpFix repair, $300+ with labor). At this price point, repairs are cost-justified given the 12-15 year design life and strong performance.

### Score Justification
The 91 score reflects best-in-class drying performance and strong reliability, offset by the persistent control board issue and recent quality concerns on newer production. It delivers premium results without premium pricing penalties.
