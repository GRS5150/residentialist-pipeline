### What You Should Know
The TOTO Ultramax II represents the gold standard in conventional toilets, combining the proven TORNADO FLUSH technology with premium one-piece construction. This toilet consistently earns the highest recommendations from professional plumbers who install it in their own homes, backed by Consumer Reports top ratings for flushing performance, bowl cleaning, and owner satisfaction.

The Ultramax II delivers MaP 1000g flush performance—the highest certification available—while maintaining water efficiency. The CeFiONtect glaze provides long-term cleanliness and antimicrobial properties that installers confirm for longevity. US manufacturing in Georgia ensures reliable parts availability and supports the domestic supply chain.

### Platform Disclosure
TOTO Ultramax II shares the TORNADO FLUSH engine with the Neorest, Drake II, Aquia IV, and other current-gen TOTO models. The Ultramax II is the one-piece version of the Drake II platform (which is two-piece). Same flush mechanism, same CeFiONtect glaze, same trapway design. Ultramax II = Drake II + one-piece construction + skirted bowl + SoftClose seat included.
