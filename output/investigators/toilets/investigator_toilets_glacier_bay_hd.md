### What You Should Know
This is a $100-150 builder-grade toilet that represents the absolute minimum acceptable quality in the residential market. While it will function as a toilet, expect frequent maintenance issues including running toilet problems from the 2-inch rubber flapper, weak flush performance, and potential cracking in the thin ceramic construction. The only genuine advantage is that when things break — and they will — replacement parts are universally available and inexpensive through standard Fluidmaster components.

Professional plumbers consistently identify Glacier Bay as generating the most service calls in their experience, making this a false economy for most homeowners. The low purchase price will be offset by higher maintenance costs and earlier replacement needs compared to mid-tier alternatives.
