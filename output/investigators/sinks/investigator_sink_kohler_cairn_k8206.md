### What You Should Know
This is Kohler's premium composite sink entry with a sophisticated matte finish and excellent resistance to daily kitchen abuse. The Neoroc material performs well in current testing but lacks the decades of field validation that Blanco Silgranit provides. If you're building a Kohler ecosystem kitchen, the parts compatibility is unmatched. The proprietary material composition means you're trusting Kohler's engineering more than proven industry formulas.

### Repair Economics
Composite sinks rarely fail structurally — primary maintenance is removing metal transfer marks from cookware contact, typically addressed with Bar Keepers Friend ($3-5) or similar cleaners.
