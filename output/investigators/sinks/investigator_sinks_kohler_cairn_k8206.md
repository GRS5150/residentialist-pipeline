### What You Should Know
The Kohler Cairn represents Kohler's premium entry into composite undermount sinks, featuring their proprietary Neoroc material that delivers excellent resistance properties in a refined matte finish. Customer satisfaction is strong at 4.6/5 stars, with users consistently praising the finish quality, drainage engineering, and ease of maintenance [SRC-004]. The engineered drainage slope is particularly well-executed, addressing a common performance issue in undermount installations.

However, Neoroc is a relatively new material with 8-10 years of market presence versus established competitors like Blanco's Silgranit with 35+ years of field data [SRC-003]. While no material failures are reported, the shorter track record means less long-term performance data. The proprietary formula also provides less composition transparency compared to competitors' documented material content [SRC-002].
