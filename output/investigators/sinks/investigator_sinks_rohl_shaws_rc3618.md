### Company Background
Rohl is owned by Fortune Brands Innovations (NYSE: FBIN), a $4.6B public company with strong financial position and no going-concern risk [SRC-007]. The Shaws line is manufactured at the historic Shaws of Darwen factory in Lancashire, England, which has operated continuously since 1897 [SRC-001]. This is genuine artisanal manufacturing, not badge-engineering — each sink is individually formed and kiln-fired by English craftsmen using traditional methods [SRC-001]. Fortune Brands also owns Moen, Perrin & Rowe, and other premium kitchen/bath brands, positioning Rohl as their luxury fireclay specialist [SRC-007]. The manufacturing heritage and individual production process distinguish this from mass-produced Chinese fireclay imports [SRC-002].

### What You Should Know
This is the gold standard of fireclay farmhouse sinks, handcrafted using 127-year-old English manufacturing methods that produce an heirloom-quality product. The extreme kiln temperatures (2100°F+) create a non-porous ceramic body that resists virtually all kitchen damage — stains, scratches, heat, and chemicals cannot penetrate the surface [SRC-001, SRC-004]. Professional kitchen designers consistently rank Shaws at the top of the fireclay hierarchy for good reason [SRC-002].

However, this excellence comes with installation requirements that distinguish it from mainstream sinks. At 140 pounds, it demands reinforced cabinetry and professional installation — this isn't a weekend DIY project [SRC-003, SRC-005]. The flat bottom, while contributing to the clean aesthetic, does retain water slightly. These are minor trade-offs for what Consumer Reports and long-term owners confirm is exceptional performance [SRC-003, SRC-006].
