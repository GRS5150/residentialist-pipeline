### What You Should Know
Sub-Zero represents the gold standard in built-in refrigeration, backed by an unprecedented 38-year track record of zero dead-on-arrival units at major retailers. The dual-compressor system means your refrigerator and freezer operate independently — if one fails, the other keeps running, and temperature precision stays within half a degree. You're paying premium pricing, but you're getting NASA-derived air purification that actually extends produce freshness, vacuum-sealed magnetic doors that create restaurant-grade seals, and a full stainless steel interior that won't absorb odors or stain over decades.

The company has been family-owned since 1945 with no private equity involvement, which translates to genuine long-term thinking about parts availability — they stock components for units over 20 years old. The 5-year full warranty and 12-year sealed system coverage is the longest standard protection in the built-in category. If you're installing a Sub-Zero in 2023 or later, you'll get the new variable-speed compressor technology that reduces energy consumption and extends equipment life even further.

### Repair Economics
Most common failure mode is ice maker malfunction; modular design allows replacement for $200-400 in parts plus service. Compressor failures are rare but cost $800-1200 total when they occur after 15+ years. Given the longevity record, repairs are consistently cost-justified.
