### What You Should Know
Sub-Zero represents the pinnacle of built-in refrigeration, with professional-grade performance that justifies its premium positioning. The dual-compressor architecture eliminates the compromises found in single-compressor systems, providing independent climate control that prevents cross-contamination between refrigerator and freezer zones. The 2023+ models have transitioned to variable-speed Split Climate™ technology, reducing energy consumption and extending compressor lifespan while maintaining the proven Embraco compressor foundation on legacy models.

The company's commitment to long-term support is unmatched in the industry — parts remain available for units dating back to 1986, and the factory-certified technician network ensures consistent diagnostic quality. Multiple owners report 15-25+ year service lives with minimal intervention, making the initial investment cost-justifiable over the product's extended lifespan.
