### Company Background
Rheem Manufacturing is a privately held subsidiary of Paloma Industries, a Japanese conglomerate. Founded in 1925, Rheem has operated as a stable, mature brand for nearly a century. The company is not publicly traded, eliminating equity market volatility concerns. Manufacturing occurs in Fort Smith, Arkansas [S5]. As a subsidiary of Paloma Industries, Rheem benefits from deep financial backing and low bankruptcy risk. This is an established mainstream brand, not a premium repositioning or recent acquisition.

### What You Should Know
This unit represents the Toyota Camry approach to HVAC — reliable, serviceable, but not exciting. The two-stage Copeland compressor is proven technology that will provide better humidity control than cheaper single-stage alternatives. At 65 dBA, it operates at moderate noise levels.

The real value proposition is serviceability. Any HVAC contractor can work on this system using standard 24V thermostats, and Rheem's warranty claim process consistently receives top marks from contractors. Universal parts availability means you won't be held hostage to proprietary components or specialized dealers.

While the 19 SEER2 efficiency rating meets current standards, it's not impressive compared to higher-end alternatives. This unit establishes a quality floor for your home without premium features or cutting-edge efficiency.
