### Strengths
- **Professional Installation Quality**: Fine Homebuilding contractors with thousands of installations praise the windows for arriving "square out of the box," reducing installation complexity and field corrections.
- **Proven Longevity**: Customer reviews document positive performance from coastal installations over 15 years, suggesting real-world durability in harsh environments.
- **Solid Performance Specs**: Laboratory-confirmed air infiltration under 0.2 CFM/ft² for standard sizes meets ENERGY STAR criteria across most U.S. climate zones.

### Deficiencies
- **Chronic Hardware Problems**: Multiple sources confirm a pattern of missing screws and hardware components requiring post-delivery remediation by Andersen shipping parts.
- **Size-Dependent Performance Drop**: Larger Tilt-Wash units achieve only DP40 versus DP50 for smaller Woodwright versions, with documented performance degradation as size increases.
- **Seal Failure Litigation**: Active class-action lawsuit alleges seal failures in eleven windows within four years, causing mold growth, with complaints that Andersen's fixes only slow deterioration.

### What You Should Know
This is a competent mid-range window that contractors trust for consistent manufacturing quality and ease of installation. The performance specs are solid for standard sizes, and the 20-year glass seal warranty provides meaningful protection. However, you're buying into a pattern of missing hardware that Andersen addresses reactively rather than preventing, and larger window sizes perform measurably worse than smaller ones. The ongoing seal failure lawsuit suggests some units may develop serious moisture problems within the warranty period.

### Score Justification
The 69/100 reflects a product that delivers on its core promise with some notable execution flaws. Professional praise for manufacturing consistency and proven field longevity prevent a lower score, while recurring hardware issues and documented performance degradation in larger sizes prevent it from reaching the mid-70s.
