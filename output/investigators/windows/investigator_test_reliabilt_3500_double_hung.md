## ReliaBilt 3500 — Investigator Analysis
**Locked Score: 28/100 — Below Standard**

### Score Decomposition
| Axis | Score | Assessment |
|------|-------|------------|
| Quality | 22 | Poor manufacturing precision with widespread delivery defects and out-of-square units |
| Performance | 35 | Basic energy specs meet minimum standards but air infiltration exceeds budget category norms |
| Durability | 27 | Premature seal failures and component wear patterns emerging within months of installation |
| Material Safety | Good | Standard vinyl construction with no identified health concerns |

### Strengths
- **Value positioning**: Professional reviewers acknowledge it delivers "decent" performance for its $450-$600 installed price point, making it accessible for budget-conscious buyers
- **Energy basics covered**: Low-E argon glass and DP-35 rating (upgradeable to DP-50) meet minimum energy efficiency requirements for replacement windows

### Deficiencies
- **Manufacturing quality control**: Customer reports document consistent patterns of windows arriving out-of-square with visible gaps and damaged screens requiring multiple replacements
- **Premature component failure**: Seal failures reported within five months, sticking operations after two years, and progressive weatherstripping degradation requiring owner workarounds
- **Service complexity**: Official manufacturer tutorials highlight "non-trivial service complexity" for basic maintenance, with customers reporting 10+ hour service escalations over three months

### What You Should Know
This window hits basic energy standards at a budget price but suffers from significant quality control issues. Professional reviewers explicitly state it's "designed for affordability not excellence" and "not recommended for primary residences." Multiple customers report receiving defective units and experiencing premature failures. The manufacturer's own service videos suggest ongoing maintenance will be more complex than typical windows.

### Score Justification
The 28/100 score reflects a product that technically functions but falls well short of reliable performance standards. Poor manufacturing precision prevents a lower score, while meeting basic energy specs prevents complete failure.
