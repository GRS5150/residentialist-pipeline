### Company Background
Loewen is a Canadian premium window manufacturer operating from a single factory in Steinbach, Manitoba. The company maintains direct control over production quality through this consolidated manufacturing approach, positioning itself as a premium alternative to mainstream US brands like Andersen and Marvin. However, this Canadian-based operation creates market friction for US customers, including extended lead times (8-16 weeks), limited parts availability at local distributors, and warranty service coordination challenges between US homeowners and the Canadian factory [SRC-004]. The company has lower US market penetration compared to dominant American manufacturers, resulting in less readily available technical documentation and reduced builder familiarity in the US market [SRC-001, SRC-004].
