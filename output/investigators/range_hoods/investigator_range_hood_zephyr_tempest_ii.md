### CFM-to-BTU Assessment
Internal blower at 650 CFM handles up to 65,000 BTU ranges adequately. External blower configuration reaches 1200+ CFM, suitable for professional-style ranges up to 120,000 BTU. No CFM-to-BTU mismatch concerns across typical residential applications.

### Strengths
- **External blower capability transforms performance**: BLB series external blower reaches 1200+ CFM with near-silent hood operation, solving the noise-vs-power tradeoff that plagues most hoods
- **Strong designer adoption in luxury segment**: 26 luxury listing sightings places Zephyr among most frequently specified brands alongside Wolf and Vent-A-Hood
- **HVI-certified performance**: Independent verification of CFM and sone ratings for both internal and external blower configurations provides reliable performance data

### Deficiencies
- **China manufacturing limits construction quality**: Body gauge is thinner than Vent-A-Hood or Wolf, and overall construction is adequate but not premium caliber
- **Limited service ecosystem**: Parts availability is direct from manufacturer only, not widely stocked at independent distributors, and customer service can be slow
- **Standard centrifugal motor**: Internal motor is conventional design that will wear faster than premium ball-bearing or Magic Lung systems

### What You Should Know
The Tempest II is Zephyr's sweet spot of design and performance, positioned as the modern alternative to traditional ventilation brands. The external blower option (BLB series) is the real value proposition — it moves the motor outside, dramatically reducing kitchen noise while boosting CFM to 1200+. With internal blower, you get competent 650 CFM performance but moderate noise levels. Kitchen designers specify Zephyr for clients wanting contemporary aesthetics with strong ventilation performance at a lower price than Wolf or Vent-A-Hood.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is centrifugal motor replacement at $200-350 in parts, plus $150-250 labor (total $350-600). With external blower configuration, the hood-side components rarely fail since the motor is relocated outside. Given the $1,500-2,500 typical purchase price, major repairs are worth completing rather than replacement.

The 82/100 score reflects strong performance capabilities (especially with external blower) and solid design quality, but falls short of Tier 1 due to standard motor construction and thinner body gauge than premium competitors. Performance dominance at 87 points drives the overall score above quality and durability concerns.
