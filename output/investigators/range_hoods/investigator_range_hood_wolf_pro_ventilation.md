### CFM-to-BTU Assessment

### Strengths
- **Centrifugal blower with ball bearings**: Premium motor design provides reliable airflow with proper bearing technology for longevity
- **Ecosystem integration**: Purpose-built CFM-to-BTU matching with Wolf ranges plus factory-certified service network
- **External blower option**: Remote/in-line blower capability adds installation flexibility for noise reduction

### Deficiencies
- **Traditional baffle filter system**: Unlike Vent-A-Hood's centrifugal capture, grease can migrate past filters to reach ductwork and blower over time
- **Higher noise levels**: Consumer Reports testing shows louder operation than Vent-A-Hood at comparable CFM settings
- **Shorter warranty coverage**: 2-year full warranty vs Vent-A-Hood's lifetime motor warranty

### What You Should Know
Wolf Pro Ventilation is engineered as part of the Wolf cooking ecosystem, not a standalone hood choice. If you're buying Wolf ranges, the matching ventilation guides ensure proper CFM-to-BTU pairing. The centrifugal blower with ball bearings is premium engineering, but the traditional baffle filter system requires monthly cleaning and allows some grease migration over time. External blower options help with noise, but internal operation is louder than Vent-A-Hood. This is a luxury appliance with luxury service support and luxury pricing.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is centrifugal blower motor replacement ($400-600 parts, $800-1200 total with labor). Proprietary Wolf parts are available through Sub-Zero/Wolf service network but expensive. Given the premium price point and 2-year warranty, major repairs within 10 years are worth doing. After 10+ years, replacement becomes more economical unless part of a full Wolf kitchen ecosystem.

### Score Justification
The 91/100 reflects genuine Tier 1 performance with premium materials and engineering, but falls short of Vent-A-Hood's 95+ territory due to traditional filtration compromises and shorter warranty coverage. The Wolf ecosystem integration justifies the premium tier placement.
