### Platform Disclosure
**MANDATORY BSH DISCLOSURE**: Thermador shares core platform with Bosch and Gaggenau under BSH Home Appliances. Star Burner and ExtraLow features are Thermador-exclusive, but underlying engineering DNA is shared across BSH brands. Range oven section likely shares components with Thermador wall ovens (confirmed cavity/hinge sharing in Masterpiece line).

### Strengths
- **Star Burner Technology**: Patented 5-arm flame geometry with raised pedestal base delivers superior flame distribution and easier cleaning compared to standard sealed burners
- **Steam Assist Functionality**: Genuine steam injection via reservoir during traditional cooking modes — differentiating feature for bread baking and moisture retention in roasts
- **BSH Service Ecosystem**: Widely stocked parts availability and national factory-authorized service network through BSH distribution

### Deficiencies  
- **ExtraLow Simmer Limitation**: Cycling gas on/off to achieve ~100°F lacks the precision of Wolf's continuous 500 BTU flame for delicate sauces
- **Standard Gas Broiler**: Missing infrared broiler advantage found in Wolf and BlueStar ranges
- **Short Warranty Coverage**: 1-year full warranty trails Wolf's 2-3 year coverage despite premium pricing

### What You Should Know
The Pro Grand combines Thermador's signature Star Burner technology with steam assist — two features that genuinely differentiate it from basic pro-style ranges. The 18K BTU output and true convection deliver solid performance, while BSH's platform ensures reliable parts availability. However, the ExtraLow simmer cycles rather than providing continuous low flame, and the standard gas broiler lacks infrared intensity. At this price point, you're paying for the Star Burner engineering and steam functionality.

### Repair Economics
**Primary repair**: Spark ignition system (surface burners). Moisture or debris causes continuous clicking — common service call averaging $150-250. BSH parts widely stocked through independent distributors. Repair cost-justified given appliance value, but Wolf's longer warranty provides better coverage window.

### Score Justification
Strong 83 reflects genuine engineering differentiators (Star Burner, steam assist) within BSH's reliable platform, but ExtraLow cycling and standard broiler prevent higher performance scoring. Durability benefits from BSH ecosystem but suffers from short warranty relative to Wolf competition.
