### Platform Disclosure
**MANDATORY BSH Platform Sharing:** Bosch 800 shares BSH induction platform core with Thermador and Gaggenau — same E.G.O. German coils, same Infineon IGBTs, same Schott CERAN glass. KEY DIFFERENCE: Bosch uses simpler 4-channel fixed/flex zone architecture while Thermador Freedom's full-surface 48-56 element array is unique hardware NOT shared with Bosch. This platform sharing explains why Bosch (1.7% service rate) significantly outperforms Thermador (4.9%) — same foundation, but Freedom adds 4x the electronic complexity.

### Strengths
- **Best-in-category reliability**: 1.7% service rate beats Thermador (4.9%) and category average (~3.8%) due to simpler 4-channel electronics versus complex multi-zone arrays
- **Premium German components**: E.G.O. coils, Infineon IGBTs, and Schott CERAN glass match what Thermador uses but in a more reliable architecture
- **Superior service ecosystem**: BSH parts universally stocked with every independent technician capable of service — strongest parts availability in induction category

### Deficiencies
- **Documented control failure pattern**: 74HC4053 triple analog multiplexer IC failure causes unresponsive touch controls, requiring full control board replacement and voiding warranty for component-level repair
- **Mid-tier power output**: 3700W maximum falls short of premium induction performance (Thermador Freedom's 4600W) limiting high-heat cooking capability
- **Basic interface design**: Touch slider controls lack the sophistication of full touchscreen systems found on higher-end models

### What You Should Know
This cooktop delivers the reliability story you want — best service rate in the induction category at 1.7%. You get genuine German premium components (same suppliers as Thermador) but in a simpler, more reliable package. The flex bridging works well for large cookware, though maximum power trails true premium models. The documented touch control IC failure affects a small percentage given the excellent overall service rate. BSH's parts ecosystem means any technician can service this unit nationwide.

### Repair Economics
**Primary failure**: Control board replacement due to 74HC4053 multiplexer IC failure. Control board costs $200-300, labor $150-200, total repair $350-500. At this price point ($1,500-2,000 typical), repair is cost-justified. BSH's wide parts availability keeps costs reasonable compared to boutique brands.
