### Platform Disclosure
Navien is manufactured by KD Navien Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Kyungdong Group in Seoul, South Korea. Kyungdong is a major HVAC/heating equipment conglomerate. Navien operates as a dedicated tankless water heater brand in the US market without cross-brand rebadging.

### Strengths
- **Dual stainless steel heat exchangers**: Avoids copper's vulnerability to acidic condensate corrosion, representing superior materials engineering for long-term durability in condensing applications.
- **Built-in ComfortFlow recirculation system**: Integrated pump with buffer tank eliminates cold water sandwich effect without requiring external equipment, a genuine convenience advantage.
- **Industry-leading flow capacity**: 11.2 GPM maximum flow rate is the highest in the calibration set, with excellent modulation range (13,300–199,900 BTU).

### Deficiencies  
- **Conditional heat exchanger warranty**: The 15-year warranty drops to just 5 years with uncontrolled recirculation — a material restriction that could catch homeowners off-guard.
- **Complex electronics vulnerability**: More sophisticated control systems and built-in pump create additional failure points compared to simpler tankless designs.
- **Korean manufacturing distance**: Parts and service support depends on trans-Pacific supply chains, though Navien has built stronger US distribution as market share has grown.

### What You Should Know
This is a premium condensing tankless with the highest flow rate in its class and sophisticated recirculation technology. The dual stainless heat exchanger design is genuinely superior to copper-based competitors for longevity. However, you're paying for complexity — the built-in ComfortFlow system and advanced controls create more potential failure points than simpler designs. The warranty structure heavily favors controlled recirculation use, so understand those terms before installation.

### Repair Economics
Primary failure mode is scale buildup requiring annual flushing ($200-300 professional service). Control board failures ($400-600) are more expensive than simpler tankless units but still cost-justified versus replacement. The built-in recirculation pump adds a failure point but costs $300-400 to replace versus full unit replacement.

### Score Justification
The 91 reflects genuine engineering excellence with dual stainless construction and industry-leading flow capacity, balanced against complexity risks. The conditional warranty structure prevents a higher durability score despite premium materials.
