### What You Should Know
This is the floor of the toilet market — a $100-150 builder-grade fixture designed to meet the absolute minimum requirements for new construction and budget renovations. The performance data tells the story: when MaP scores exist at all, they typically fall well below premium standards, and many models simply aren't tested because the manufacturer doesn't invest in third-party validation.

Quality control reflects the price point, with thin-wall china construction that's prone to cracking and lacks the specialized glazing that prevents staining over time. The plumbing community consensus is clear — these generate more service calls than any other toilet category, primarily for clogs, valve failures, and the infamous running toilet syndrome that comes with budget-grade internal components.

The one saving grace is parts compatibility. When (not if) components fail, standard Fluidmaster parts fit perfectly and cost under $20 at any hardware store. This accessibility makes ongoing maintenance feasible, which is fortunate because you'll be doing it regularly.
