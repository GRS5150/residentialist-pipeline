### Corporate Disclosure
Therma-Tru is owned by Fortune Brands Home & Security, providing manufacturing stability and integrated supply chain capacity at their Butler, Indiana facility.

### Strengths
**Premium Construction Quality**: Compression-molded fiberglass slab with high-density polyurethane foam core (1.9 lbs/ft³) delivers superior thermal resistance (K-factor 0.15) compared to polystyrene or fiberglass alternatives.

**Comprehensive Hardware Package**: Multi-point locking system standard on all heights with ball-bearing hinges and sophisticated medium-reach weatherstrip system featuring 7-shape corner seal pads.

**Strong Warranty Coverage**: Lifetime transferable warranty on door slab with 10-year coverage on PrismaGuard finish, backed by major corporate parent.

### Deficiencies
**Jamb System Weakness**: Professional contractors report finger-jointed white pine jamb stock deteriorating within years where it meets aluminum thresholds, contradicting rot-resistant marketing claims.

**Thermal Expansion Vulnerability**: Field reports document multiple slab replacements due to thermal expansion failures behind storm doors, with inner and outer skins expanding at different rates causing weatherstrip failure.

**Maintenance Dependencies**: PrismaGuard finish requires reapplication every 3-5 years with specific temperature and humidity requirements, and warranty excludes doors behind storm doors or within 5 miles of salt water.

### What You Should Know
This is genuinely premium fiberglass construction with excellent thermal performance and quality hardware, but the gap between marketing and field reality is concerning. The jamb failures contradict the "never rot like wood" promise, and thermal expansion issues suggest careful installation planning is critical. The warranty exclusions for storm doors and coastal environments limit protection for common applications. Factor in realistic maintenance expectations — this isn't truly "maintenance-free" despite marketing claims.

### Channel & Availability
Available through authorized dealers and select big-box retailers. Professional installation strongly recommended given strict finishing requirements (all six surfaces must be finished within specific timeframes for warranty validity).

### Score Justification
The 91 score reflects genuinely superior construction quality and thermal performance, but durability concerns and warranty limitations prevent a perfect score. The documented field failures and thermal expansion issues are real engineering problems that affect long-term value despite the premium construction.
