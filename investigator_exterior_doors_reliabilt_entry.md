### Corporate Disclosure
Reliabilt is manufactured by JELD-WEN exclusively for Lowe's home improvement stores. This is a cost-optimized product line designed for big-box retail distribution rather than professional dealer channels.

### Strengths
- **Multi-point locking system** comes standard, providing three-point security engagement (top, middle, bottom) that exceeds basic single-deadbolt configurations
- **Polyurethane foam core** delivers R-10 to R-12 thermal resistance through closed-cell insulation structure, meeting basic energy efficiency needs
- **24-gauge galvanized steel construction** provides corrosion resistance adequate for most climate zones through zinc barrier coating

### Deficiencies
- **Bonded-frame construction** uses adhesive bonding instead of welding at door perimeters, creating documented failure modes under impact loading where steel edges separate from door skin
- **Quality control variability** in weatherstripping installation requires field supplementation and verification during installation to achieve acceptable air infiltration
- **Absent NFRC certification** prevents independent verification of air infiltration performance claims, limiting transparency compared to certified competitors

### What You Should Know
This is JELD-WEN's budget line made exclusively for Lowe's, not their premium product. The bonded construction cuts manufacturing costs but creates weak points that professional installers report failing under impact. Expect to verify and possibly supplement the weatherstripping during installation. The multi-point locking is a genuine security upgrade, but the structural compromises make this suitable only for budget applications where you accept trade-offs in long-term reliability.

### Channel & Availability
Available exclusively at Lowe's home improvement stores with typical big-box lead times. This is a cost-optimized retail product, not the same specifications as JELD-WEN's dealer-channel doors.

### Score Justification
The 40/100 reflects a product that meets basic function but exhibits concerning quality compromises. The bonded construction and quality control issues prevent higher scoring despite adequate thermal performance and security features.
