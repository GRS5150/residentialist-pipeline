## FRAME MATERIAL EVALUATION

### Aluminum Exterior Cladding
**Status: No concern.**

The extruded aluminum exterior is finished with Pella's EnduraClad Plus — a 70% fluoropolymer resin multi-step baked-on finish applied to the exterior surface. Factory-cured fluoropolymer coatings are chemically stable and inert post-cure. Aluminum itself has no off-gassing concern. The exterior cladding is not in contact with interior air in any meaningful way. No flags from any healthy homes source for installed aluminum cladding.

### Wood Interior (Pine / Select Softwood)
**Status: Minor concern — finish chemistry unverified.**

The wood interior is the evaluation focus for aluminum-clad wood products per the knowledge file. Per the [Pella Architect Series Architectural Design Manual](https://detroitmi.gov/sites/detroitmi.localhost/files/hdc-submitted-materials/2021-05/Pella%20Catalog%20-%20window%20sections.pdf), the wood frame is select softwood (pine, mahogany, or douglas fir) immersion-treated with EnduraGuard® wood protection formula.

**EnduraGuard treatment:** EnduraGuard contains "three active ingredients for protection against moisture, decay, stains from mold and mildew, plus an additional ingredient for termite protection." This is a proprietary biocide treatment. The specific active ingredients are not publicly disclosed in product documentation. EnduraGuard is applied to the wood before assembly — it is a factory wood preservative/fungicide/termiticide treatment.

**Finish options:** The Architect Series offers interior surfaces in:
- Prefinished stain or paint (pine)
- Primed, ready-to-paint (pine)
- Unfinished (Contemporary and Traditional styles)

Factory-applied stains, paints, and primers on wood window interiors may contain VOCs. No VOC content specification or low-VOC claim is documented for Pella's interior finish products in publicly available materials.

**Assessment without certification:** Per the knowledge file, "No cert, wood interior, established brand" = 8.5–9.0 starting range. Pella is unambiguously an established premium manufacturer (Big Three US window manufacturer, in continuous operation since 1925, US-manufactured). Moderate confidence that factory finish chemistry meets standard low-VOC industrial practice, but this is unverified by third-party testing.

**Corinne Segura investigation flag — wood treatment chemistry:** Segura's article ["Least Toxic Window Frames"](https://www.mychemicalfreehouse.net/2023/09/least-toxic-window-frames-aluminum-wood-fiberglass-or-vinyl.html) notes that standard wooden windows are "usually treated with fungicides, pesticides, and insecticides" and that glue in conventional wood windows could be irritating for sensitive individuals. She recommends aluminum-clad wood windows for those who want wood aesthetics. **Segura does not identify Pella specifically as a concern** — her observation applies to the wood window category generally.

*Per knowledge file Corinne Segura / My Chemical-Free House Special Rule (Item 12-C):* This is a category-level observation, not a Pella-specific finding. Segura's general concern about wood treatment chemistry is noted here as an investigation flag. It does not meet the corroboration threshold for a scored finding against this product specifically. **No score impact applied.** Monitoring note: undisclosed biocide chemistry in EnduraGuard warrants attention if Segura or a second Tier 1 source identifies specific concern with Pella's wood treatment formulation.

## GLAZING CHEMISTRY

**Status: No concern.**

Standard assessment applies:
- **Argon gas fill:** Inert noble gas. No health concern.
- **Low-E coating:** Microscopic metallic oxide layer bonded to glass surface; not in contact with interior air. No exposure pathway. No concern.
- **Sealed IGU spacer and edge seal system:** Dual-seal insulating glass unit with silicone or polyisobutylene secondary seal. No active flags from healthy homes community for installed, sealed IGU units.
- **Polyurethane Reactive Hotmelt (PUR) glazing** (on triple-pane models): Factory-cured; inert post-cure. No consumer exposure concern.

Glazing chemistry is not a differentiating factor for any standard Pella dual- or triple-pane unit.

## INSTALLATION FOAM ADVISORY

**⚠ YELLOW ADVISORY — Universal (Rule 13)**

This advisory applies to all window installations and is installation-dependent, not product-dependent. **No score reduction applied.**

> Request your installer use pre-cured backer rod + sealant systems at interior-accessible surfaces, or ensure a minimum 24–48 hour ventilation period before occupancy if spray polyurethane foam is used for gap fill during installation. Spray polyurethane foam off-gasses MDI (methylene diphenyl diisocyanate) and amine catalysts during curing — both are respiratory irritants. Post-cure foam is inert and poses no ongoing concern.

## INTERIOR FINISH

**Status: Unverified — moderate confidence in low-VOC practice based on manufacturer tier.**

Factory-applied interior finishes (prefinished stain, paint, primer) on the Architect Series wood interior are not covered by any third-party emissions certification. Pella does not publish VOC content specifications for its interior finish products in public-facing documentation.

**Contextual assessment:**
- Pella is a premium, US-manufacturing-based window brand with established industrial process controls.
- The knowledge file notes that "established premium manufacturers (Marvin, Andersen, Sierra Pacific) use factory-applied low-VOC finish systems as standard practice. Moderate confidence without specific cert."
- Pella is in the same manufacturer tier as the listed brands. Moderate confidence that factory-applied finishes meet standard low-VOC industrial practice — but this is inferred from manufacturer position, not verified by testing.
- Greenguard Gold certification, if held, would explicitly cover factory-applied finish VOC emissions. Its absence leaves this at moderate-confidence unverified status.

**Buyer note on finish selection:** The "unfinished" option (available on Contemporary and Traditional styles) eliminates factory finish chemistry entirely. Homeowners with chemical sensitivity who select unfinished Architect Series can then apply their own verified low-VOC finish, giving greater control over interior air quality.

## MATERIAL SAFETY SCORE

**8.5 / 10 — Rated (No Certified Score Available)**

### Score Justification

**Starting position:** "No emissions cert, wood interior, established brand" = 8.5–9.0 per knowledge file certification floors table.

**Score set at 8.5** (lower end of the range) for the following reasons:

1. **No Tier 2 emissions certification (Greenguard Gold, SCS Indoor Advantage Gold, or equivalent).** This is the primary reason the score cannot reach 9.0 or above. Andersen holds SCS Indoor Advantage Gold. JELD-WEN holds Greenguard Gold on many lines. Pella's decision not to pursue emissions certification for the Architect Series leaves the interior finish and wood treatment chemistry unverified by independent testing. This is a gap, not a finding — but it prevents a higher score.

2. **EnduraGuard biocide chemistry is undisclosed.** Three active ingredients are referenced in architectural specifications without identification. The wood preservative/fungicide/termiticide treatment is applied factory-applied to the interior wood framing. Without ingredient disclosure, the specific chemistry cannot be evaluated against the ILFI Red List or Pharos chemical hazard database. This contributes to moderate uncertainty at the margins.

3. **No active healthy homes flags from any Tier 1 or Tier 2 source.** No credible source has identified a specific health concern with the Pella Architect Series. This prevents any score reduction below the certification floor.

4. **Pella is an established premium US manufacturer.** This supports moderate confidence in standard low-VOC factory practices. The Architect Series is positioned as Pella's premium product line, supporting the inference that finish quality standards are at or above industry norms.

**What would raise the score:**
- Greenguard Gold certification covering frame and factory-applied finishes → 9.5
- SCS Indoor Advantage Gold or CA 01350 certification → 9.0–9.2
- Independent indoor air quality test report (not Greenguard-certified, but documented) → 9.0
- Full EnduraGuard ingredient disclosure and Red List screening → 9.0

**What would lower the score:**
- Confirmed Tier 1 source finding specific VOC concern with Pella Architect interior finishes → 5.0–7.0
- Confirmed Red List ingredient in EnduraGuard → reassess

## BUYER GUIDANCE

The Pella Architect Series Casement is an established premium product from a major US manufacturer with no active health flags from any credible healthy homes source — but it lacks third-party emissions certification (Greenguard Gold or equivalent) that competitors Andersen and JELD-WEN hold, leaving interior finish and wood treatment chemistry unverified.
