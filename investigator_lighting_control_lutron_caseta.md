### Strengths
- **Clear Connect RF superiority**: Operates on dedicated frequency band, eliminating the connectivity dropouts plaguing WiFi switches competing with network traffic
- **"Set it and forget it" reliability**: Smart Bridge uptime approaches 100% with consistent user reports of zero maintenance required
- **LED compatibility infrastructure**: Lutron maintains tested database of hundreds of LED models, providing solutions when compatibility issues arise

### Deficiencies
- **75-device hard limit**: Inadequate for whole-home lighting in larger properties, making this unsuitable for luxury specifications
- **Basic switch aesthetics only**: Limited to Diva/Claro styles — no access to Lutron's premium Palladiom or seeTouch keypads available in higher-tier systems
- **Single point of failure**: Smart Bridge dependency means total system failure if the hub goes down

### What You Should Know
Caseta is Lutron's entry-level system that introduces homeowners to the Clear Connect ecosystem before upgrading to RadioRA 3 or HomeWorks. It's the most recommended smart lighting system by reviewers specifically because its proprietary RF protocol eliminates the reliability issues common with WiFi switches. The 75-device limit makes it perfect for condos and smaller homes but inadequate for whole-home specification. You get Lutron's 8-year warranty and legendary reliability, but none of the premium aesthetics or advanced features found in their professional systems.

### System Architecture Note
Clear Connect Type A wireless operates on a proprietary RF frequency separate from WiFi, Bluetooth, and Zigbee. This dedicated spectrum prevents the network congestion that causes connectivity dropouts with internet-dependent smart switches. The Smart Bridge acts as the central hub, creating a mesh network that extends throughout the home.

### Score Justification
The 64 reflects Caseta's position as reliable entry-level Lutron — exceptional durability through Clear Connect RF, but quality limitations in aesthetics and device capacity that prevent higher scoring. It's genuinely good at what it does, just constrained by design for the DIY market segment.
