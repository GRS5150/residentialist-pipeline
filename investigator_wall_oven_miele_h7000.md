### Platform Disclosure
No evidence of platform sharing found. Miele manufactures in Gütersloh, Germany with vertical integration.

### Strengths
- **TwinPower dual-fan convection system**: True European convection with dedicated ring element and dual fans provides exceptional temperature uniformity and faster cooking
- **Reliability leadership**: 9.6% first-year service rate from Yale Appliance data places Miele among the most reliable wall oven brands
- **M Touch controls with guided cooking**: Intuitive touchscreen interface with automated programs reduces user error and optimizes results

### Deficiencies
- **Pyrolytic self-clean durability concern**: High-temperature cleaning (up to 900°F) creates component stress that can affect long-term reliability despite strong first-year performance
- **Premium parts ecosystem**: German-manufactured components likely command higher replacement costs when repairs are needed outside warranty period

### What You Should Know
The H7000 represents Miele's flagship wall oven technology with dual-fan convection that outperforms single-fan systems in temperature uniformity. Professional installers consistently rank Miele at the top alongside Wolf for cooking performance and reliability. The steam assist feature genuinely improves bread baking and roasting results. While the 9.6% service rate is excellent, the intensive pyrolytic self-cleaning may create long-term component stress. This is a premium product that delivers on its promises but requires commitment to Miele's service ecosystem.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair: control board/ERC failure (industry-wide wall oven pattern). Miele's German manufacturing means premium parts pricing, with control boards likely $400-600 and total repair costs $600-900 with labor. Given the 95-point score and premium positioning, repairs are cost-justified through warranty period and reasonable for 8-10 years of ownership.

### Score Justification
The 95/100 reflects near-perfect execution with one meaningful tension: Performance and Quality excellence offset by Durability concerns from pyrolytic self-cleaning stress. The tight Yale service rate spread (8.8-10.5%) means Miele's 9.6% represents strong but not perfect reliability leadership.
