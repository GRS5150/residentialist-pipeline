### Platform Disclosure
Rinnai Corporation (TSE: 5947) is a single-brand Japanese manufacturer with no rebadging or sub-brands in the US market. Engineering done in Nagoya, Japan; US assembly at company-owned Griffin, Georgia facility.

### Strengths
**Dual stainless steel condensing technology** — Primary and secondary heat exchangers capture both combustion and latent heat, achieving 0.95 UEF efficiency while resisting acidic condensate corrosion that destroys copper-based systems. **Unconditional 15-year heat exchanger warranty** — No conditional terms based on recirculation type, unlike Navien's 15yr/5yr split warranty structure. **Established US parts and service network** — Rinnai PRO Network certification program ensures trained technicians and consistent parts availability through authorized distributors.

### Deficiencies
**No built-in recirculation pump** — Circ-Logic compatibility requires external pump installation, adding cost and complexity versus Navien's integrated ComfortFlow system. **Mandatory annual flushing requirement** — Scale buildup on heat exchanger will void warranty if maintenance is skipped, creating ongoing homeowner obligation. **Control board electronics** — PCB failures documented after 8-10 years represent the primary non-maintenance failure mode.

### What You Should Know
This is a 20+ year investment if you maintain it properly. The condensing technology captures waste heat that non-condensing units vent outside, translating to real energy savings. You'll need annual flushing (about $150-200 professionally) to prevent scale damage. The Japanese engineering and US assembly model gives you precision manufacturing with domestic service support. Expect excellent hot water performance for multiple simultaneous fixtures.

### Repair Economics
Scale-related heat exchanger damage is the #1 repair ($800-1,500), making annual flushing ($150-200) cost-justified. Control board replacement ($400-600) after 8-10 years is still economical versus replacement given the 20+ year expected lifespan.

### Score Justification
The 93/100 reflects genuine best-in-class engineering with the condensing efficiency cliff (0.95 vs 0.79 UEF), premium dual stainless construction, and unconditional warranty terms. Points lost for lacking integrated recirculation and requiring strict maintenance discipline.
