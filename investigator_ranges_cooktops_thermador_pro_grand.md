### Platform Disclosure
**MANDATORY BSH PLATFORM SHARING**: Thermador shares core range platform with Bosch and Gaggenau. Star Burner design and ExtraLow cycling are Thermador-exclusive features, but underlying engineering DNA is BSH common architecture. Thermador Pro Harmony uses the same platform at lower specification.

### Key Components
**Burner System**: Star Burner with 5-arm flame geometry (BSH-designed), gas valves likely Copreci (Spanish BSH OEM partner). **Igniter**: Standard spark ignition for burners, hot-surface igniter for oven. **Convection**: True European single-fan convection with dedicated element.

### Strengths
- **Star Burner flame distribution**: 5-arm geometry reduces cold spots compared to standard sealed burners, with raised pedestal base for easier cleaning access
- **Steam-assist functionality**: Genuine steam injection via reservoir during traditional cooking modes provides moisture retention advantage for bread baking and roasting
- **BSH parts ecosystem**: Widely stocked components through national distribution with searchable E-Nr model numbers and established service network

### Deficiencies
- **ExtraLow simmer limitation**: Cycling on/off system achieves ~100°F but lacks precision of Wolf's continuous 500 BTU flame floor for delicate sauces
- **Standard gas broiler**: Gas standard broiler cannot match infrared heat density of Wolf or BlueStar systems for steakhouse-quality searing
- **Single-fan convection**: True convection but single-fan system shows 6-10°F temperature variance versus Wolf VertiCross dual-fan architecture

### What You Should Know
This is a premium gas range with BSH engineering reliability and Thermador's distinctive Star Burner flame pattern. The steam-assist oven feature genuinely differentiates it for serious baking. However, the ExtraLow simmer cycles rather than maintaining continuous low flame, making it less suitable for prolonged sauce work than Wolf. Like all gas ranges, it requires proper ventilation to address NO₂ emissions. The 1-year warranty is shorter than Wolf's 2-3 year coverage.

### Repair Economics
Most common repairs: spark igniter failure ($150-250 installed), oven hot-surface igniter ($200-300), gas valve issues ($300-450). Control board failure less common but expensive ($400-600+). BSH parts availability is excellent with wide distributor network. At this price point, most repairs under $500 are cost-justified; major component failures approaching $800+ may warrant replacement consideration.

### Score Justification
The 83/100 reflects genuine premium engineering with BSH reliability heritage, but stops short of Tier 1 due to simmer cycling limitation and single-fan convection. The Star Burner and steam assist provide clear differentiation from commodity ranges, justifying the Excellent rating within Tier 2.
