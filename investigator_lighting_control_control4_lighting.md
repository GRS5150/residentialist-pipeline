### Strengths
- **Comprehensive automation platform**: OS 3 provides unified interface integrating lighting with security, climate, and entertainment systems beyond pure lighting control
- **Broad device compatibility**: Strongest third-party device support in the category through established dealer channel and 22+ year track record
- **Custom keypad options**: Dealer-programmable engraved keypads provide personalized control interfaces

### Deficiencies
- **Spectrum interference vulnerability**: Zigbee operates on shared 2.4 GHz band susceptible to interference from WiFi devices, baby monitors, and microwaves unlike Lutron's dedicated frequency
- **Corporate instability**: Two ownership changes (Snap One 2019, Resideo 2024) with dealers reporting product development delays and uncertainty
- **Shortest warranty**: 2-year standard warranty significantly shorter than Lutron's 8-year coverage

### What You Should Know
Control4 excels as a whole-home automation platform where lighting is one integrated component rather than the primary focus. The system offers excellent third-party device compatibility and custom programming through dealers. However, the Zigbee mesh can experience interference in homes with many wireless devices, and recent corporate changes have created uncertainty about long-term support and development.

### System Architecture Note
Uses Zigbee mesh networking where every powered device acts as a signal repeater, creating redundant communication paths. However, Zigbee operates on the crowded 2.4 GHz spectrum shared with WiFi and other devices, making it more susceptible to interference than dedicated-frequency systems.

### Score Justification
The 67 score reflects Control4's strength as an automation platform with solid lighting capabilities, but acknowledges that lighting is not its core specialization. Corporate instability and spectrum interference concerns prevent a higher tier ranking despite strong integration capabilities.
