### Company Background
Brizo is owned by Masco Corporation, a publicly-traded Fortune 500 company that also owns Delta Faucet and acquired Brizo as their luxury brand positioning. Manufacturing has undergone significant migration with Delta now producing no more than one-third of Brizo faucets in the US, with the remainder manufactured in China [SRC-005]. This manufacturing shift caused StarCraft to reclassify Brizo from North American to Asian luxury category [SRC-001]. The brand shares key components across the Masco ecosystem, including the proprietary DST cartridge manufactured at Delta's Morgantown Kentucky plastics plant and PVD finishes from Masco subsidiary Vapor Technologies Inc [SRC-002, SRC-003]. Masco's financial backing ensures strong parts availability and warranty infrastructure, though the brand has lost its premium domestic manufacturing positioning.

### Strengths
- **DST Cartridge Engineering**: Diamond Seal Technology cartridge independently tested to 5 million cycles—10x ASME standard—with diamond-dust embedded discs that self-polish and eliminate mineral deposits [SRC-002]
- **Brilliance PVD Finishes**: Lifetime-guaranteed finishes withstand drain cleaners, 100+ household cleaning products, and steel wool scouring without corrosion, tarnishing, or discoloration [SRC-003]

### Deficiencies
- **Manufacturing Quality Inconsistency**: Not all Brizo models use DST cartridge—some use Sedal cartridges from China or unmarked cartridges of unknown quality and origin [SRC-004]
- **Plastic Spray Wands**: All kitchen spray wands use plastic construction, positioning below competitors like Waterstone that use metal wands [SRC-007]

### What You Should Know
Brizo represents a tale of two product lines within the same brand. Models equipped with DST cartridges offer genuinely exceptional engineering—the cartridge is independently tested to last approximately 700 years of daily use, and the PVD finishes are among the most durable in the industry. However, manufacturing migration means over two-thirds of production now occurs in China, and critically, not all Brizo models include the premium DST cartridge that justifies the luxury pricing.

Before purchasing any Brizo faucet, you must verify DST cartridge presence, as StarCraft explicitly recommends this verification step [SRC-001]. Non-DST models may include inferior cartridges of unknown quality and origin. Professional plumbers rate DST-equipped models highly and find cartridge replacement straightforward, though some specific Litze models present set screw accessibility challenges [SRC-010]. The comprehensive lifetime warranty and Masco's parts infrastructure provide reassurance, but the plastic spray wands on kitchen models represent a notable compromise at the luxury price point.
