### Platform Disclosure
**GE Platform Sharing**: Café wall ovens share the Selmer TN factory platform with GE Monogram, Profile, and standard GE models. Internal components, heating elements, and control systems are largely identical across the GE portfolio, with differentiation primarily in exterior styling and interface design.

### Strengths
- **Superior first-year reliability**: 8.8% service rate leads Yale Appliance's measured brands, outperforming Miele (9.6%) and Bosch (9.7%)
- **Robust service network**: GE's national service infrastructure provides strong warranty execution and parts availability
- **True convection system**: Dedicated convection element delivers consistent heating performance across cavity

### Deficiencies  
- **Limited premium features**: Spring-loaded hinges instead of soft-close, partial-extension racks rather than full ball-bearing systems
- **Platform commoditization**: Shared internal architecture with lower-tier GE models reduces differentiation to cosmetic elements
- **Steam capability gap**: Lacks advanced steam assist features found in Miele, Wolf, and Gaggenau competitors

### What You Should Know
GE Café delivers solid wall oven performance with the best measured first-year reliability in Yale's database. The "chef-inspired" branding is primarily cosmetic — internally, you're getting the same proven GE platform used across their entire wall oven lineup. While it lacks premium features like steam cooking and soft-close hinges, the strong service network and proven reliability make it a sensible choice for homeowners prioritizing dependability over luxury features.

### Repair Economics
Most common repair is control board/ERC failure (~$300 parts, $150 labor). Heating elements run $80-150 plus labor. Total repair costs typically $200-450, which is cost-justified given the unit's reliability track record and GE's parts availability. The shared platform strategy means parts remain available and affordable.

### Score Justification
The 65/100 reflects strong reliability performance lifting an otherwise middle-tier product. Durability scores high due to the Yale service data leadership, while Quality and Performance remain moderate due to feature limitations and platform sharing that prioritizes cost over differentiation.
