### Company Background
TOTO Ltd, founded in 1917, is a Japanese public company (TSE: 5332) and the world's largest plumbing fixture manufacturer. The company operates US manufacturing facilities in Morrow, Georgia, providing domestic supply chain advantages and supporting local employment. TOTO maintains premium brand positioning through engineering innovation rather than cost competition, with consistent profitability and strong financial health. The company dominates the luxury toilet segment globally while expanding into mid-market residential through products like the Ultramax II. Distribution runs primarily through professional plumbing supply houses including Ferguson and SupplyHouse, ensuring proper installation support.

### What You Should Know
The TOTO Ultramax II represents the conventional toilet benchmark, combining TORNADO FLUSH technology with CeFiONtect glazing at a workhorse price point. This is the toilet that plumbers install in their own homes, offering MaP 1000g flush performance in a one-piece skirted construction. Consumer Reports consistently rates TOTO models as top performers for flushing, bowl cleaning, and overall owner satisfaction [A2].

The Ultramax II provides better long-term durability prospects than TOTO's electronic models because it uses standard flapper and fill valve components rather than proprietary electronics. US manufacturing in Georgia ensures parts availability and reduces supply chain risk compared to imported alternatives.

### Platform Disclosure
TOTO Ultramax II shares the TORNADO FLUSH engine with the Neorest, Drake II, Aquia IV, and other current-gen TOTO models. The Ultramax II is the one-piece version of the Drake II platform (which is two-piece). Same flush mechanism, same CeFiONtect glaze, same trapway design. Ultramax II = Drake II + one-piece construction + skirted bowl + SoftClose seat included.
